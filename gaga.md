# Gaga

Stefani Joanne Angelina Germanotta, known professionally as Lady Gaga, is an American singer, songwriter, and actress celebrated for her theatrical performances and genre-blending pop music. Emerging with the album *The Fame* in 2008, she quickly became a global icon through hit singles like "Just Dance" and "Poker Face."

## Musical evolution
- *The Fame Monster* and *Born This Way* established her as a pop innovator, blending synth-pop, dance, and rock influences while addressing identity, self-acceptance, and inclusivity.
- *Artpop* explored electronic experimentation, *Joanne* introduced stripped-back instrumentation, and *Chromatica* returned to dance roots, pairing high-energy production with themes of healing and resilience.
- Collaborative projects such as *Cheek to Cheek* and *Love for Sale* with Tony Bennett showcased her jazz versatility and vocal range.

## Cultural impact
- Known for boundary-pushing fashion and performance art, Gaga uses visual storytelling to amplify messages about individuality and self-expression.
- She champions LGBTQ+ rights, mental health awareness, and anti-bullying initiatives, leveraging her Born This Way Foundation to support youth empowerment and kindness campaigns.
- Accolades include multiple Grammy Awards, an Academy Award for "Shallow," and recognition for her philanthropic work, highlighting her influence across music, film, and social advocacy.
