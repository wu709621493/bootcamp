# NSF Funding Equipment Purchase Triple-Sign Pass

## Purpose

The **triple-sign pass** is a three-party authorization workflow for equipment purchases made with National Science Foundation (NSF) funding. It is designed to ensure that purchases are:

1. **Scientifically justified** for the funded scope of work,
2. **Administratively compliant** with institutional procurement and NSF award conditions, and
3. **Financially allowable** under budget and cost-principle rules.

## The Three Required Signatures

### 1) Principal Investigator (PI) Sign-off
- Confirms the equipment is required to execute approved project aims.
- Verifies the specification is right-sized (not over-scoped or unrelated).
- Attests that no adequate shared/core alternative is available at lower total cost.

### 2) Department/Unit Administrative Sign-off
- Confirms procurement pathway (bid/quote thresholds, sole-source justification, vendor eligibility).
- Confirms timeline, receiving logistics, and inventory tagging requirements.
- Ensures local policy alignment (capitalization thresholds, service contracts, warranties).

### 3) Sponsored Research/Grants Finance Sign-off
- Confirms allowability and allocability to the NSF award.
- Verifies budget category fit (equipment vs. supplies, participant support exclusions, etc.).
- Confirms remaining obligated balance and any prior approval requirement.

## Minimum Documentation Packet

A triple-sign pass should include:
- Purchase request form (item description, quantity, cost, vendor).
- Scientific justification linked to specific award objectives.
- Price support (quotes, market comparison, or sole-source memo).
- Cost allocation note if shared use is expected.
- Compliance checklist (export controls, cybersecurity, safety, facilities constraints).

## Pass / Hold Decision Rules

### Pass (Approved)
All three signatures are present, documentation is complete, and no compliance blocker is open.

### Hold (Pending)
One or more signatures missing, documentation incomplete, or clarifications required (e.g., budget rebudgeting, revised quote, or prior approval request).

### Reject (Not Approved)
Request is out of scope, unallowable, duplicates existing capacity without justification, or violates sponsor/institutional policy.

## Recommended Turnaround Standard

- **Standard requests:** decision within 5 business days.
- **Complex/high-value requests:** decision within 10 business days, with explicit issue list.

## Audit Readiness Notes

To maintain a defensible record, retain all sign-off artifacts, vendor communications, and receiving confirmations in the award file. Ensure fixed-asset records and project charge entries reconcile with the approved triple-sign pass packet.
