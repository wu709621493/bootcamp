# Sweet Potato Den

A small, lantern-lit nook where the air smells like caramelized roots and wood smoke. The Sweet Potato Den is a quiet refuge for cold evenings—a place built around one humble ingredient and the many ways it can be warmed, softened, and sweetened.

## The Atmosphere
- Soft light, low tables, and a slow simmer in the corner.
- Ceramic bowls set over cork trivets to keep the warmth close.
- A gentle soundtrack: crackling coals, steeping tea, and the scrape of a wooden spoon.

## Signature Preparations
- **Ember-roasted wedges:** Skins blistered, flesh fluffy, finished with a pinch of flaky salt.
- **Sweet potato congee:** Silky rice porridge with cubes of orange flesh, ginger, and a ribbon of sesame oil.
- **Charred mash:** Whipped with brown butter, a splash of cream, and toasted pepitas for crunch.
- **Maple glaze:** A quick reduction of maple, soy, and citrus zest brushed over hot slices.

## Serving Ritual
1. Warm bowls with hot water and dry them well.
2. Spoon in the base—mash, congee, or roasted wedges.
3. Add contrast: toasted seeds, herbs, or a drizzle of oil.
4. Finish with something bright—lemon zest, scallions, or chili flakes.

## Small Notes from the Den
- The best sweetness comes from patience: roast low and slow.
- Keep the skins on for texture and depth.
- Pair with something tart to sharpen the finish.

The Sweet Potato Den is less a recipe and more a promise: warmth, comfort, and a soft landing at the end of the day.
