# Municipal Road Quick Flattening Injection

## Concept
Municipal Road Quick Flattening Injection (MRQFI) is a rapid maintenance method for smoothing localized road defects (ruts, shallow potholes, utility-cut edges) with a pumpable, fast-curing filler.

## Goal
- Restore drivability within hours instead of days.
- Reduce traffic disruption in dense urban corridors.
- Extend pavement life by stopping water intrusion early.

## Operational Workflow
1. **Survey and mark** damaged segments with depth and area thresholds.
2. **Mill and clean** only unstable material around each defect.
3. **Inject leveling compound** under controlled pressure to fill voids and re-level the surface.
4. **Top-seal and texture** for skid resistance and water shedding.
5. **Reopen lane** after rapid cure verification.

## Candidate Material Profile
- Two-part polymer-modified mineral slurry.
- Low shrinkage and high early compressive strength.
- Compatible thermal expansion with surrounding asphalt.
- Moisture-tolerant adhesion promoter.

## Municipal Deployment Notes
- Prioritize bus routes, intersections, and emergency corridors.
- Run at night with mobile lane closures and compact crews.
- Track each patch with GPS and follow-up inspections at 7, 30, and 180 days.

## Performance Metrics
- Mean roughness reduction (before/after IRI).
- Rework rate within 6 months.
- Average lane-closure duration.
- Cost per square meter versus conventional patching.

## Risks and Mitigations
- **Bond failure in wet substrate** → mandatory moisture testing and primer use.
- **Over-injection heave** → pressure limits and staged filling.
- **Cold-weather cure delays** → winter formulation and thermal blankets.

## Pilot Recommendation
Launch a 12-week pilot across three roadway classes (arterial, collector, local). Compare against standard patching on safety, ride quality, and lifecycle cost to determine scale-up readiness.
