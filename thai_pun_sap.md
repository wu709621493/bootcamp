# Thai Pun Sap

A short Thai-style pun sequence that leans on rhyme, tone, and playful double meanings.

1. **ปลา-ป่า**: *Pla* (fish) meets *pa* (forest) — “A fish walks into a forest: **pla pa**.”
2. **ข้าว-เข้า**: *Khao* (rice) vs. *khao* (enter) — “To enter, eat rice: **khao khao**.”
3. **ใจ-ไจ**: *Jai* (heart) and *jai* (satisfy) — “A heart that satisfies: **jai jai**.”
4. **หนาว-นาว**: *Nao* (cold) and *nao* (lime) — “Cold day, lime tea: **nao nao**.”
5. **ดี-ตี**: *Dee* (good) and *tee* (hit) — “Good at rhythm: **dee tee**.”

These puns rely on Thai tonal differences that can collapse into the same romanization, creating playful ambiguity.
