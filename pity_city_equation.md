# Pity + City = (P+C)ity

Pity on its own is soft and singular, often an inward sigh. City on its own is loud and plural, a chorus of streets. When they are added together—Pity + City—the parentheses gather both and carry them forward as **(P+C)ity**: a place where empathy and infrastructure overlap.

- **P** stands for the private pulse that notices another person’s strain.
- **C** is the civic canvas of crosswalks, clinics, community kitchens.
- The combined word holds both, suggesting that compassion should scale the way buildings do: rising in stories, wired into the grid.

A city without pity is brittle; pity without a city is isolated. Join them and you get public tenderness that can be zoned, budgeted, and built into daily life.
