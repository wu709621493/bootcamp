# Schizophrenic P vs NP

> A surreal dialogue in two minds about one question.

## Voice P
I am the clock-face mind.
I march in polynomial footsteps,
never skipping a beat:

- Input arrives.
- Time bends, but not too far.
- Answer lands before the sun cools.

I trust construction.
If a maze exists, I can map it.
If a lock exists, I can machine the key.
I am certainty with a schedule.

## Voice NP
I am the thunder-after-thought mind.
I do not promise to build quickly,
but show me a finished key,
and I will know in a blink whether it sings.

- Guess first.
- Verify fast.
- Repeat in the dark until dawn.

I am witness, not always architect.
I am the judge of answers,
not the swift maker of them.

## Chorus
Inside one skull, the voices argue:

**P:** "If you can check it quickly, maybe you can find it quickly."

**NP:** "Maybe. Or maybe discovery is a mountain and checking is a fingerprint."

**P:** "Then why do we feel so close?"

**NP:** "Because every proof is a mirror,
and mirrors make distance look small."

## Coda
So the question remains,
not broken, not healed:

\[
\textbf{P} \overset{?}{=} \textbf{NP}
\]

Two voices.
One unsolved sentence.
A universe listening.

---

*Note: The title uses "schizophrenic" in an artistic, split-voice sense, not as a medical description.*
