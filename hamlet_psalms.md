# Hamlet P(s)alms

A cycle of meditations blending Hamlet's soliloquies with psalm-like cadences.

## I. Midnight Ramparts
- **Invocation:** "To see or not to see"—a watchman's prayer beneath the aurora of Elsinore.
- **Supplication:** Ghostly whispers drift like incense; fear becomes a choir of unanswered petitions.
- **Restraint:** The sword stays sheathed; mercy trembles but does not break.

## II. Chamber of Mirrors
- **Confession:** Doubt tallies its debts in the silvered glass; my reflection kneels beside me.
- **Lament:** Each corridor repeats a question; the echo is its own psalmist.
- **Resolution:** I vow to let conscience sing until the ceiling cracks.

## III. Players' Psalm
- **Prelude:** A trumpet of memory summons actors who hold lanterns to my grief.
- **Psalmody:** Their lines carry the salt of Denmark; their pauses are holy rests.
- **Offering:** I place a script upon the altar of public gaze, inviting truth to set fire to deceit.

## IV. Cloister of Ophelia
- **Watermark:** Lilies scribble slow cursive along the moat; sorrow floats but will not drown.
- **Intercession:** I ask the reeds to pray for gentleness, to braid soft armor around fragile minds.
- **Gloria:** Even the river keeps a hymn for those the castle could not cradle.

## V. Graveyard Canticle
- **Memento:** A skull weighs less than an oath, yet more than a crown.
- **Silence:** Shovels measure the rests between breaths; mortality hums a ground note.
- **Amen:** The dust receives us like an unfinished verse—faith trusts the cadence to resolve.

## VI. Final Benediction
- **Ascension:** The stage empties; the audience holds its breath like a cathedral of quiet lungs.
- **Peace:** I hand my doubts to the horizon and receive a new name: witness.
- **Dismissal:** Go in unrest turned to rest; let tragedy rehearse itself no more.
