# Da Vinci Helicopter

Leonardo da Vinci sketched a vertical-lift concept often called the “aerial screw,” a helical rotor meant to push against the air and rise when turned. The idea relied on a spiraling sail of linen or reed, stiffened by a framework, and would be powered by human operators walking a platform to spin the screw.

While the design could not fly with the materials and power available in the 15th century, it is notable for anticipating the core principle of rotorcraft: generating lift through a rotating, angled surface. Today it is remembered as a visionary experiment in vertical flight and a precursor to helicopter engineering.
