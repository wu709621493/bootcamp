# To Pray

Praying is an intentional pause that gathers attention, gratitude, and hope into a single quiet moment. Below is a simple guide to preparing and offering a prayer, followed by a short example.

## Preparing to Pray
- **Find stillness:** Choose a calm place, silence devices, and take three slow breaths.
- **Name your intent:** Clarify whether you are offering thanks, asking for guidance, or holding someone in care.
- **Center on gratitude:** Recall one concrete thing you are thankful for today.
- **Hold humility:** Acknowledge limits and openness to learn or be changed.

## A Simple Structure
1. **Opening:** Address the divine, your conscience, or the quiet beyond you.
2. **Thanks:** Name specific moments or people you appreciate.
3. **Request:** Ask for wisdom, healing, courage, or peace—something you are willing to work toward.
4. **Commitment:** State one small action you will take in alignment with your request.
5. **Closing:** End with a phrase of release, such as “Amen,” “Let it be,” or “I release this.”

## Breath Prayer (One-Line)
- Inhale: *“Receive peace.”*
- Exhale: *“Share peace.”*

## Example Prayer
> Quiet Presence, thank you for the breath in my lungs and the people who steadied me today. Please give me courage to speak kindly and act justly. I will check on a friend this evening and listen with care. Let it be so.

## After the Prayer
- Sit in silence for a few breaths before moving on.
- Carry one word from your prayer—like **peace**, **courage**, or **gratitude**—as a touchstone for the day.
