# Protective Jealousy

Protective jealousy is the instinct that flares when something precious feels threatened. It is not the same as possession; it is a signal that care is awake, that boundaries and belonging matter. At its best, it asks us to defend what is shared, to guard trust, and to keep a relationship safe from erosion.

## Key Points
- **Alarm, not verdict:** It alerts us to perceived risks, but it does not prove them.
- **Care with limits:** Protection becomes harmful when it turns into control or surveillance.
- **Shared responsibility:** Both people can name the vulnerability and co-create boundaries.
- **Rooted in value:** The strength of the feeling often mirrors the depth of commitment.

Protective jealousy can be wise when it leads to clarity and connection. It becomes fragile when it tries to substitute fear for trust. The aim is to protect the bond, not to imprison the people inside it.
