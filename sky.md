# Sky

The sky is a canvas of distance and light that refuses ownership. It turns sunrise into a lesson on patience, noon into a reminder of clarity, and night into an invitation to listen beyond our walls.

## Signals we can read
- **Scale.** Looking up humbles the metrics we use for ambition; there is always more room than we imagine.
- **Change.** Clouds redraw the horizon without apology, showing that revision is part of any honest story.
- **Shelter.** Even storms carry protection—the promise that water will return to soil and that thunder warns before it arrives.

## How to keep it in sight
- Pause between tasks and notice the gradient above the buildings.
- Let your eyes rest on a moving cloud when decisions feel stuck.
- Remember that wherever we stand, the same sky covers strangers and friends alike, making every conversation a little closer to universal.
