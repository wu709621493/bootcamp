# Electric 2-Wheeler Park Plan (6,464 m²)

## Objective
Design a dedicated electric 2-wheeler parking and charging park within **6,464 square metres** that is safe, efficient, and scalable.

## Space Program (Recommended Allocation)

| Zone | % of Total Area | Area (m²) | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Parking bays + circulation aisles | 58% | 3,749 | Core vehicle movement and parking |
| Charging infrastructure zone | 14% | 905 | Slow/fast chargers, power cabinets |
| Access, entry/exit, queuing | 8% | 517 | Gate buffers, ticketing/smart access |
| Utilities and battery safety area | 6% | 388 | Transformer, fire systems, isolation space |
| Admin, control room, amenities | 5% | 323 | Office, waiting, restrooms |
| Green buffer + stormwater + walkways | 9% | 582 | Shade trees, drainage, pedestrian comfort |
| **Total** | **100%** | **6,464** |  |

## Capacity Estimate

### Typical footprint assumptions
- Standard electric scooter/motorbike stall: **1.0 m × 2.0 m = 2.0 m²**
- Effective per-vehicle area including aisle share and maneuvering: **3.8–4.5 m²/vehicle**

### Approximate parking capacity
- Using 3,749 m² (parking + circulation) and 4.0 m²/vehicle average:
  - **~930 vehicles**
- Practical operating range after buffers, safety clearances, and mixed sizes:
  - **~780 to 900 vehicles**

## Charging Mix Proposal
- **70% slow AC charging points** (overnight/long dwell)
- **25% medium AC/DC points** (2–4 hour turnover)
- **5% fast charging points** (high-turnover use)

For 800 active parking slots:
- Slow points: ~560
- Medium points: ~200
- Fast points: ~40

> Not all stalls must be wired from day one; trunk conduits and panel capacity should be sized for full future electrification.

## Suggested Layout Logic
1. **Two primary one-way loops** for conflict-free movement.
2. **Clustered charging islands** every 40–60 bays to reduce cable runs.
3. **Dedicated fast-charge edge zone** near entry for quick in/out.
4. **Pedestrian-only spine** connecting entry, admin, and waiting areas.
5. **Fire break lanes** and clear emergency access around utility blocks.

## Safety and Compliance Essentials
- Thermal event response protocol and quarantined parking pocket.
- Fire detection + suppression designed for Li-ion battery incidents.
- Non-combustible canopy materials and cable management overhead/guarded.
- IP-rated chargers, anti-slip paving, and drainage away from electrical assets.
- 24/7 CCTV, smart metering, access control, and emergency shutoff points.

## Sustainability Features
- Solar canopy on parking rows to offset daytime demand.
- Rainwater harvesting for landscape and cleaning use.
- Native shade trees to reduce surface heat and rider discomfort.
- LED smart lighting with motion or schedule-based dimming.

## Phasing Strategy
- **Phase 1 (60% build-out):** ~480–540 operational bays with core utilities.
- **Phase 2 (85% build-out):** add medium/fast chargers and expanded circulation controls.
- **Phase 3 (100% build-out):** full capacity and advanced load management (demand response, battery storage integration).

## Quick Feasibility Snapshot
A **6,464 m²** site can support a high-efficiency electric 2-wheeler park with roughly **780–900 practical parking capacity** (depending on final geometry and code requirements), while preserving safe movement, charging resilience, and future growth.
