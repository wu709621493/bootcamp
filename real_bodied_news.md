# Real-bodied News

## Definition

**Real-bodied news** is reporting that keeps a full human body in frame: facts, context, consequences, and lived experience.

It avoids two distortions:

1. **Floating-head news**: isolated quotes with no material context.
2. **Spreadsheet-only news**: metrics without people, places, and stakes.

## Core principles

- **Verifiable facts first**: identify what is known, unknown, and contested.
- **Context with proportion**: place events in historical, legal, and economic background.
- **Human impact**: show who is affected and how, including downstream effects.
- **Source transparency**: describe methods, evidence quality, and limits.
- **Correction discipline**: update quickly and visibly when errors are found.

## Practical checklist

Before publishing, ask:

- What happened?
- How do we know?
- Who benefits, who pays, and who is missing from this story?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What would falsify this claim?

If these five questions are answered clearly, the piece is usually more complete, more trustworthy, and more useful to readers.
