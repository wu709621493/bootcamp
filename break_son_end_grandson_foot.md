# Break Son End Grandson Foot

A son breaks a branch to mark the trail,
so he can find his way home before dark.
Years later, his grandson finds that scar,
now wrapped in bark and birdsong,
and asks why anyone would wound a tree.

The old man smiles and taps his foot on the soil.
"I thought I was leaving a sign for one night,"
he says,
"but time turned it into a question for you."

Every break becomes an ending for something,
and a beginning for someone else.
A footstep is never only a footstep;
it is a message pressed into earth,
waiting for another generation to read it.
