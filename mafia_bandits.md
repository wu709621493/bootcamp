# Mafia Bandits

The Mafia Bandits are a legendary crew of thieves who mix the strict discipline of old-world families with the improvisational flair of street banditry. Each member takes an oath called *Il Patto Ombra*—a vow to never leave a teammate behind and to always redistribute a portion of the haul to the neighborhoods they grew up in.

## Structure and Roles
- **Capo Stratega**: Plans the heists and negotiates with allied crews, drawing maps on napkins that later become framed art in the safe house.
- **Occhio Lungo (Long Eye)**: A watcher who knows every security rotation in the city and can spot a change in guard posture from three blocks away.
- **Cuore Silente (Silent Heart)**: The stealth expert who can bypass alarms with a musician's sense of timing.
- **Pilota di Fumo (Smoke Driver)**: Handles getaways using decoy routes and smoke canisters that smell faintly of citrus to confuse scent-tracking drones.

## Code of Conduct
1. **Honor over hoard**: Reputation is worth more than the take.
2. **No civilian scars**: They plan routes to avoid collateral harm.
3. **Give back quietly**: After every major job, they repair a community landmark anonymously.

## Notable Heist: The Midnight Tram Switch
A bank transfer was hidden inside a midnight freight tram. The Mafia Bandits rerouted the tram through a disused line, unloaded the cargo while the city slept, and returned the train before dawn. No one noticed until a week later, when a mural appeared on the tram depot wall depicting a wolf carrying a briefcase.

## Rumored Safe Houses
- **The Citrus Garage**: Smells like oranges from the smoke canisters; doubles as a mechanic's shop.
- **Belladonna Library**: A private collection of banned books where plans are written in invisible ink between the lines of classic novels.

## Cultural Impact
Local street musicians have adopted the crew's rhythms, tapping out alarm bypass patterns on steel drums. Neighborhood kids trade homemade cards depicting the crew's members, each with stats like "lockpick tempo" and "getaway drift angle." The Mafia Bandits may be outlaws, but in their city, they're also unlikely folk heroes.
