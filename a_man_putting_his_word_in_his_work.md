# A Man Putting His Word in His Work

He enters the shop before the day has spoken,
sets his promises beside the tools.
Each nail remembers what his mouth once said,
each board is squared to the measure of his truth.

He does not chase applause, only alignment—
the vow and the labor, the tongue and the hand.
He sands the lie away, he seals the seams,
lets the grain of his character show.

By dusk, his word is standing in the doorway,
quietly holding up the light.
