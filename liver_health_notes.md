# Liver Health Notes

Maintaining liver health combines lifestyle habits with awareness of warning signs that warrant medical attention.

## Core functions
- Filters toxins, metabolizes medications, and processes alcohol.
- Produces bile to digest fats and carry away waste.
- Stores glycogen, vitamins, and minerals for energy balance.
- Regulates cholesterol and supports blood clotting factor production.

## Everyday support
- Keep alcohol intake within medical guidelines or abstain entirely if advised.
- Prioritize balanced meals with fiber-rich vegetables, lean proteins, and healthy fats.
- Stay hydrated to aid metabolic processes and bile flow.
- Aim for consistent physical activity to support weight and insulin sensitivity.
- Avoid unnecessary supplements or medicines that stress the liver; follow dosing instructions.

## Protective habits
- Practice safe sex and avoid sharing needles or personal items to reduce viral hepatitis risk.
- Keep vaccinations current where recommended (e.g., hepatitis A and B).
- Use protective gear and proper ventilation when handling solvents or fumes.

## Signs to seek care
- Persistent fatigue, nausea, right upper abdominal discomfort, or loss of appetite.
- Jaundice (yellowing skin or eyes), dark urine, pale stools, or unexplained bruising/itching.
- Swelling in legs or abdomen, confusion, or sleep-wake inversion.

## When to consult professionals
- Before starting new medications, supplements, or high-intensity diets.
- If you have a history of liver disease, diabetes, obesity, or significant alcohol use.
- For regular monitoring when on long-term medications processed by the liver.

These notes complement, but do not replace, guidance from qualified healthcare professionals.
