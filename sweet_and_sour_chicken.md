# Sweet and Sour Chicken

A classic sweet-and-sour chicken with crisp, bite-sized pieces and a bright sauce that balances tang, fruitiness, and gentle heat. This version uses pantry staples and finishes quickly once everything is prepped.

## Ingredients (Serves 3–4)

### Chicken
- 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp white pepper (or black pepper)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 1/2 cup (60 g) cornstarch
- 1/2 cup (65 g) all-purpose flour
- Neutral oil, for frying (about 2–3 cups / 480–720 ml)

### Sweet and Sour Sauce
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) ketchup
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) rice vinegar
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) pineapple juice (from canned pineapple or carton)
- 1/4 cup (55 g) packed light brown sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp water

### Stir-Fry Finish
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 small yellow onion, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 green bell pepper, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 cup (165 g) pineapple chunks (fresh or canned, drained)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger

### To Serve
- Steamed rice
- Sliced scallions or toasted sesame seeds (optional)

## Instructions
1. **Prep the sauce.** In a bowl, whisk ketchup, rice vinegar, pineapple juice, brown sugar, and soy sauce until smooth. In a small bowl, whisk cornstarch with water to make a slurry.
2. **Season and coat the chicken.** Toss chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Add beaten egg and mix to coat. Sprinkle in cornstarch and flour; toss until a shaggy, evenly coated batter forms.
3. **Fry the chicken.** Heat 2 inches of oil in a heavy pot or Dutch oven to 350°F (175°C). Fry chicken in batches for 4–5 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and cooked through. Transfer to a rack or paper towel-lined sheet.
4. **Stir-fry the vegetables.** In a large skillet or wok, heat 2 tbsp oil over medium-high heat. Add onion and peppers; stir-fry 2–3 minutes until lightly blistered but still crisp. Add garlic and ginger; cook 30 seconds.
5. **Build the sauce.** Pour in the sauce mixture and bring to a simmer. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook 30–60 seconds until glossy and thickened.
6. **Combine.** Add pineapple chunks and fried chicken to the skillet. Toss to coat until everything is hot and evenly glazed, about 1 minute.
7. **Serve.** Spoon over steamed rice and garnish with scallions or sesame seeds if desired.

## Tips
- **Keep it crisp:** Fry in small batches so the oil temperature stays steady.
- **Lighter option:** Skip frying and pan-sear the chicken in 2–3 tbsp oil, then continue with the sauce.
- **Balance to taste:** Add an extra splash of vinegar for more tang or a pinch of sugar for more sweetness.
