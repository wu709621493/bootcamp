# Dana Harvard Fiber Optic Research Studio

The Dana Harvard Fiber Optic Research Studio is a conceptual interdisciplinary lab focused on high-bandwidth optical communication, photonic sensing, and next-generation fiber materials. Its work combines physics, materials science, and systems engineering to improve signal integrity over long distances, reduce energy use in data transmission, and expand practical uses for fiber optics in medicine, infrastructure monitoring, and secure networks.

A studio model emphasizes rapid prototyping: researchers move from simulation to benchtop experiments and then to field-ready demonstrations. Typical projects include low-loss waveguide design, robust connectors for harsh environments, and distributed fiber sensor arrays that can detect strain, heat, or vibration in real time. The goal is to turn photonics research into deployable tools for communication and public-interest technology.
