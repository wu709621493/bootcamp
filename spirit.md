# Spirit

Spirit is the breath that carries memory forward. It rests in the lungs when the day is quiet,
and it rises with the ribs whenever conviction stirs. Spirit is not a distant glow—it is a
current inside the body that asks us to remain present.

## Locating it
- In the pause before a reply, when patience wins over pride.
- In the weight of a hand on a shoulder, when reassurance speaks without words.
- In the determination to start again after failure, when hope chooses to be stubborn.

## Keeping it
We keep spirit alive by taking responsibility for what we touch. Caring for small routines,
sharing tools, and listening closely are simple tasks, yet they keep warmth circulating. We
feed spirit when we let curiosity stay awake longer than fear.

## Offering it
Spirit is easiest to give away. A quiet nod in agreement, a letter that arrives on time, or a
promise kept—each gesture tells someone else they are not alone in the room. When spirit is
shared, it folds into community and returns stronger.
