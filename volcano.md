# Volcano Resilience Playbook

1. **Wakefulness of the mountain**: Track seismic whispers, gas flux, and ground swelling together so alerts grow from converging lines of evidence instead of a single blinking sensor.
2. **Maps for movement**: Keep hazard maps living—updated with new flows, ash plumes, and lahar paths—then pair them with rehearsed evacuation routes that match real travel times, not ideal ones.
3. **Air and water safeguards**: Prepare filtration and catchment protocols for ashfall so hospitals, schools, and water plants can shift into protected modes within minutes, not hours.
4. **Communication scaffolding**: Run multi-lingual, low-bandwidth messaging trees that reach phone dead zones through radio relays and volunteer runners, with clear go/no-go phrases to reduce panic.
5. **Recovery muscle memory**: Stockpile modular shelters, ash-proof PPE, and small business microgrants, and drill rapid debris clearing so livelihoods restart alongside lifelines.
