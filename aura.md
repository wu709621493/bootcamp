# Aura

Aura is the subtle field of feeling and perception that surrounds our choices. These notes sketch ways to notice and nurture it in daily work.

- **Sensing presence:** Before speaking, pause to register the room's temperature—voices, posture, and breathing. That moment of notice cools reactive impulses and invites clarity.
- **Coloring intent:** Imagine each task tinted with a hue: deep blue for focus, gold for gratitude, green for growth. Associating intent with color can anchor priorities without rigid schedules.
- **Porous boundaries:** A healthy aura is neither armored nor porous to exhaustion. Practice gentle filters: say no when needed, and say yes with full attention rather than scattered obligation.
- **Resonant collaboration:** Teams share an aura when they exchange context generously—documenting decisions, narrating tradeoffs, and acknowledging uncertainty. This resonance reduces friction and prevents silent misalignment.
- **Recharging rituals:** Light, breath, and movement refresh the aura. Step outside after a long debug session, or play one calming chord before a meeting to reset tone.
- **Signal hygiene:** Digital spaces also carry aura. Curate notifications, tidy project boards, and use respectful naming. Clean signals reduce the static that drains energy.

## A short visualization

Picture a soft halo encircling your shoulders. With each steady breath it widens, letting sharp edges soften and conversations land more gently. The aura brightens when curiosity outweighs judgment.
