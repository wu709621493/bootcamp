# Three-Legged Bike

A conceptual three-legged bike explores how adding a stabilizing tripod could change balance, maneuverability, and accessibility for riders.

## Design Goals
- Offer self-standing stability at low speeds or stops.
- Retain the narrow profile and pedaling feel of a bicycle.
- Provide adaptability for delivery riders, mobility users, and urban commuters.

## Geometry and Stability
- **Tripod layout:** Two legs fold out from the rear axle, creating a three-point stance with the front wheel when deployed.
- **Center of mass:** Battery or cargo can be placed low along the downtube to reduce tip-over risk.
- **Lean dynamics:** Hinged rear legs allow limited lean so turning still feels like a bike.

## Drivetrain and Braking Options
- Chain or belt drive with internal-gear hub to keep the drivetrain clean while folding legs pivot.
- Regenerative hub motor possible on the front wheel for assisted launches when carrying cargo.
- Twin hydraulic discs, with a parking brake lever to lock the front wheel when stationary.

## Materials and Folding Mechanism
- **Frame:** 7005 aluminum or chromoly steel for weldable mounts on the rear axle cluster.
- **Legs:** Carbon or aluminum struts with rubberized feet for grip on uneven pavement.
- **Locking:** Cam-lock hinges with detents for ride, deploy, and stowed positions; one-hand lever near the saddle controls both legs via cables.

## Use Cases
- **Urban delivery:** Stable loading and unloading without a kickstand, especially on slopes.
- **Adaptive mobility:** Added confidence for new riders or those with balance challenges.
- **Family errands:** Secure stops when carrying a child seat or front basket.

## Quick Spec Snapshot
| Feature | Concept Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Wheel size | 700c or 20" cargo variant |
| Estimated weight | +1.5–2.2 kg over a standard bike |
| Max payload | 150 kg (rider + cargo) |
| Fold time | ~2 seconds with single lever |
| Storage | Legs nest along chainstays; no extra parking footprint |

## Open Questions
- How do the rear legs affect pedaling Q-factor and heel clearance?
- What’s the optimal detent force to prevent accidental deployment while riding?
- Could a suspension pivot integrate with the leg hinge to absorb bumps when deployed?

This concept aims to combine bicycle agility with tripod stability, inviting prototyping to validate ride feel and user safety.
