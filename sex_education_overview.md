# Sex Education Overview

Sex is a broad term that encompasses biological characteristics, sexual activity, and a person’s gender identity. In health education, the topic is addressed with an emphasis on consent, safety, and respect.

## Key Principles
- **Consent**: All sexual activity must be voluntary, informed, and enthusiastic. Anyone can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Respect and communication**: Partners should openly discuss boundaries, contraception options, and expectations.
- **Safety**: Using barrier methods and regular sexual health screenings helps prevent sexually transmitted infections (STIs).
- **Inclusivity**: Information should acknowledge diverse orientations, gender identities, and relationship structures.

## Resources for Learning More
- Local public health clinics for confidential advice and testing services.
- Trusted educational organizations that provide age-appropriate sexual health curricula.
- Licensed medical professionals for personalized guidance.
