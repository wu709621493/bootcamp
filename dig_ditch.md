# To Dig a Ditch

A safe, efficient trench starts with preparation and proceeds in measured phases. The following steps keep the project organized and protective of people, tools, and the surrounding environment.

## 1. Define purpose and path
- Write down the reason for the ditch (drainage, conduit, footing) and the required dimensions.
- Mark the route with high-visibility paint or string lines; measure twice before breaking ground.
- Call the local utility locator service and wait for clearance to avoid striking buried lines.

## 2. Choose tools and protective gear
- Personal protection: boots with toe protection, gloves, eye protection, hearing protection, and a hard hat where overhead hazards exist.
- Hand tools: trenching shovel, mattock or pick for hard soil, spade for edging, and a tamper for backfill.
- Optional power: a walk-behind trencher or mini-excavator for long runs; confirm training and rental agreements.

## 3. Prepare the site
- Mow or clear surface vegetation along the marked path.
- Stage soil tarps or wheelbarrows to separate topsoil from subsoil if restoration is needed.
- Establish spoil piles at least 2 feet from the trench edge to reduce collapse risk.

## 4. Break ground in controlled passes
- Start with shallow cuts along the marked line to confirm there are no unexpected obstructions.
- Dig in layers rather than full depth at once; step back walls or shore them if deeper than local safety limits.
- Keep the bottom graded toward any planned drainage outlet and check slope with a level or line.

## 5. Manage water and stability
- Pump or bail standing water promptly; never work in a trench with rising water.
- Install boards or trench boxes if depth or soil type demands shielding.
- Pause digging if you encounter roots, rocks, or suspected utilities; reassess before proceeding.

## 6. Finish and restore
- Place bedding material (sand, gravel) for pipes or conduit and check alignment before backfilling.
- Backfill in lifts, compacting each layer to reduce settlement; keep different soil layers separated if specified.
- Restore the surface with seed, sod, or pavement patch, and clean tools before storage.

## 7. Daily safety checklist
- [ ] Utility clearance confirmed and marks visible
- [ ] Weather and soil conditions reviewed
- [ ] PPE worn by all workers
- [ ] Spoil piles set back and stable
- [ ] Escape ladders provided for trenches over 4 feet deep
- [ ] Work zone isolated from vehicles and pedestrians

Documenting each stage keeps the ditch aligned with its purpose while protecting workers and the site.
