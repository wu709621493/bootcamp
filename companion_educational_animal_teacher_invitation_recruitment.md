# Companion Educational Animal Teacher Invitation & Recruitment

## Program Overview
We are launching a **Companion Educational Animal Teacher Program** to bring humane, science-based, and joyful animal-assisted learning into classrooms, libraries, and community spaces.

We invite educators, handlers, and animal welfare professionals to join us as founding teaching partners.

---

## Our Mission
To help learners build:
- empathy and emotional literacy,
- practical knowledge of animal care and behavior,
- confidence in communication and responsibility,
- respect for biodiversity and ethical coexistence.

---

## Who We Are Recruiting
### 1) Companion Animal Teacher Leads
Professionals who can co-design and deliver educational sessions with trained companion animals.

### 2) Animal Welfare & Behavior Mentors
Specialists who can ensure each session models safe, humane, and evidence-based interaction.

### 3) Curriculum Co-Creators
Educators with strengths in early childhood, K-12, special education, or community programming.

### 4) Program Volunteers / Teaching Assistants
Reliable team members who support logistics, setup, student engagement, and post-session care.

---

## Core Responsibilities
- Plan and teach age-appropriate animal education modules.
- Demonstrate compassionate handling and welfare-first care.
- Collaborate with schools/families on learning goals.
- Keep accurate records on attendance, student progress, and animal wellbeing.
- Follow all safety, hygiene, and consent protocols.
- Contribute to inclusive learning environments for diverse learners.

---

## Minimum Qualifications
- Experience in education, animal training, veterinary support, or related fields.
- Knowledge of animal stress signals and welfare standards.
- Strong communication, patience, and teamwork.
- Ability to pass required background and safeguarding checks.
- Commitment to non-aversive, humane teaching practices.

### Preferred Qualifications
- Certification in animal behavior, humane education, or therapy animal handling.
- First aid / CPR (human and/or pet first aid).
- Experience working with neurodivergent learners or trauma-informed settings.

---

## Program Values
- **Animal welfare is non-negotiable.**
- **Learner safety and dignity come first.**
- **Curiosity, kindness, and scientific thinking belong together.**
- **Every lesson should strengthen both knowledge and compassion.**

---

## Training & Support We Provide
- Orientation in educational framework and classroom facilitation.
- Safety and risk management onboarding.
- Lesson templates and assessment tools.
- Peer mentoring and reflective practice sessions.
- Ongoing professional development workshops.

---

## Session Themes (Examples)
- Reading animal body language
- Building trust through gentle interaction
- Nutrition, habitat, and enrichment basics
- Responsible guardianship and public etiquette
- Conservation connections from pets to ecosystems

---

## Time Commitment & Format
- Flexible part-time and project-based options available.
- Weekday school hours, after-school blocks, and occasional weekend events.
- In-person priority; hybrid support roles may be available.
