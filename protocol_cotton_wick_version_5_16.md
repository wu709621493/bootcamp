# Protocol Cotton Wick (Version 5.16)

Protocol Cotton Wick (v5.16) is a compact quality-control and handling procedure for cotton wicks used in capillary systems (lamps, humidifiers, and lab-scale fluid-delivery prototypes).

## Objectives

- Ensure consistent capillary rise and flow stability.
- Reduce contamination, fraying, and premature charring.
- Standardize storage, prep, and acceptance checks across batches.

## Materials and setup

- 100% cotton wick stock (document lot ID).
- Distilled water for wetting and capillary tests.
- Stainless ruler or caliper.
- Precision scale (0.01 g resolution or better).
- Nitrile gloves and sealed sample bags.

## Procedure

1. **Incoming inspection**
   - Verify fiber composition and lot number.
   - Reject rolls with visible mold, oil staining, or odor contamination.
2. **Dimensional check**
   - Cut three 100 mm samples.
   - Measure width/diameter at three points per sample; record mean and range.
3. **Mass-per-length check**
   - Weigh each 100 mm sample and compute g/m.
   - Compare with specification window for the target device.
4. **Capillary rise test**
   - Suspend each strip vertically with 10 mm immersed in distilled water.
   - Record rise height at 30 s, 60 s, and 180 s.
5. **Conditioning and packaging**
   - Dry conditioned samples in clean ambient air.
   - Seal in labeled bags with lot, date, and inspector initials.

## Acceptance criteria (v5.16 baseline)

- Dimensional variance within ±5% of nominal.
- Mass-per-length coefficient of variation ≤ 8% across three samples.
- Capillary rise at 180 s must meet or exceed device minimum spec.
- No visible lint shedding during gentle tension test.

## Storage and shelf-life

- Store at 15–27 °C, relative humidity below 60%.
- Keep away from solvents, oils, and direct sunlight.
- Recommended requalification interval: every 6 months for opened lots.

## Change notes for v5.16

- Added explicit three-timepoint capillary rise logging.
- Tightened mass variability threshold to improve flow predictability.
- Standardized bag-label fields for better batch traceability.
