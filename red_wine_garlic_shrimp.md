# Red Wine Garlic Shrimp

## Overview
A fast, weeknight-friendly shrimp dish where garlic, butter, and a splash of red wine create a glossy pan sauce. Serve with crusty bread, pasta, or roasted vegetables.

## Ingredients (Serves 2–3)
- 1 lb (450 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined (tails on optional)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to heat preference)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) dry red wine
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Instructions
1. **Season the shrimp.** Pat shrimp dry. Toss with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika.
2. **Sear.** Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer and cook 60–90 seconds per side until just opaque. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Bloom the aromatics.** Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, garlic, and red pepper flakes. Stir 30–45 seconds until fragrant (do not brown).
4. **Build the sauce.** Stir in tomato paste for 20 seconds. Add red wine and scrape up browned bits. Simmer 2–3 minutes until slightly reduced.
5. **Finish.** Return shrimp to the skillet. Add butter and lemon juice; toss to coat until butter melts and shrimp are warmed through, about 1 minute.
6. **Serve.** Sprinkle with parsley and serve with lemon wedges.

## Tips
- Use a dry, medium-bodied red like Pinot Noir, Merlot, or Tempranillo.
- If your shrimp are small, reduce sear time to avoid overcooking.
- For a richer sauce, swirl in an extra tablespoon of butter right before serving.

## Suggested Sides
- Garlic bread or toasted baguette
- Angel hair or linguine
- Roasted asparagus or broccolini
