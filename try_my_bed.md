# Try My Bed

Trying a new bed is less about the mattress itself and more about how well it fits your routines. This note outlines a quick way to evaluate a bed without needing fancy equipment.

## Quick checklist
- **Spine alignment:** Lie on your side and ask a friend to check whether your spine stays straight. If it curves, the bed may be too soft or too firm.
- **Pressure points:** Spend at least ten minutes on your back. Tingling around your shoulders or hips hints at poor pressure relief.
- **Motion isolation:** Sit down gently, then have someone else shift on the opposite side. Good beds dampen the wave quickly.
- **Edge support:** Sit on the edge while tying your shoes. If you feel like you are sliding off, the edge support needs work.

## Overnight simulation
If possible, mimic a full night. Read a book, check emails, or stretch on the bed for twenty to thirty minutes. These micro-routines reveal whether the texture, temperature, and sound of the mattress match your daily life.

## Sanitation and courtesy
When inviting someone to try your bed, provide a clean sheet or mattress protector, and give them room to move without feeling observed. A little hospitality turns a quick test into useful feedback.
