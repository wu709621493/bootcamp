# Independence

## Essence
Independence is the practiced ability to choose a course without coercion while staying accountable for the consequences. It thrives on curiosity, discipline, and the humility to revise a stance when new evidence appears.

## Personal practice
- Build routines that keep basic needs steady: rest, food, movement, and time to think.
- Learn a craft that can travel with you, giving options regardless of geography.
- Question defaults kindly; understand why rules exist before deciding whether to bend or improve them.
- Track decisions and outcomes so freedom becomes informed rather than impulsive.

## Collective independence
- Communities gain resilience when no single channel controls knowledge, tools, or resources.
- Transparent governance and shared maintenance of infrastructure reduce dependency on gatekeepers.
- Independence is reinforced by teaching others to act with agency rather than hoarding expertise.

## Interdependence
True independence avoids isolation. It is strengthened by deliberate collaborations where each party can say no, negotiate terms, and exit gracefully. Mutual respect keeps agreements healthier than fear.
