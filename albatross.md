# Albatross

The albatross is a lesson in patience and precision. With wings that lock into gliding positions, it rides ocean winds for hours without a single stroke, turning empty air into effortless travel.

## What it teaches
- **Endurance.** Long-distance foraging flights show that consistency, not speed, carries life forward over vast stretches.
- **Economy.** Dynamic soaring turns small wind shifts into propulsion, a reminder that subtle adjustments can power large journeys.
- **Care.** Albatross pairs incubate eggs in turns across harsh seasons, illustrating that responsibility is a shared, ongoing craft.

In every slow circle above the waves, the albatross invites us to value sustained attention and to conserve energy for the moments that matter.
