I, Windy Wu, being of sound mind and disposing memory, declare that this is my Last Will and Testament. I revoke all prior wills and codicils.

- **Testator:** Windy Wu ("I" or "me").
- **Immediate Family:** [Insert spouse/partner name if applicable]; children: [insert names], or if none, state "no children".

I appoint **[Primary Executor Name]** as Executor of this Will. If the primary executor is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint **[Alternate Executor Name]** as alternate.

The Executor shall have all powers permitted by law to collect, manage, sell, and distribute my estate, including operating or winding down any business interests, paying debts and taxes, and resolving claims.

## Article III: Guardianship (if minor children)
If I have minor children at my death, I nominate **[Primary Guardian Name]** as Guardian of their person and property. If the primary guardian cannot serve, I nominate **[Alternate Guardian Name]** as alternate.

   - $[amount] to [beneficiary name and relationship].
   - $[amount] to [beneficiary name and relationship].

- [Beneficiary Name] — [percentage]%
- [Beneficiary Name] — [percentage]%
- [Beneficiary Name] — [percentage]%

## Article VI: Digital Assets
I direct my Executor to take control of my digital assets (including online accounts, digital files, and cryptocurrency wallets). I authorize the Executor to access, reset passwords, manage, or delete such accounts consistent with my privacy wishes and applicable law. A separate list of accounts and instructions may be kept with this Will or in a secure location.

## Article VII: Debts, Taxes, and Expenses
I instruct that all enforceable debts, funeral expenses, and administrative costs be paid from my estate. Any estate or inheritance taxes shall be paid from the residue unless a specific bequest states otherwise.

## Article VIII: Pets (if applicable)
I leave the care of my pets to **[Pet Caretaker Name]** and direct my Executor to provide $[amount] from my estate to fund their care. If this caretaker cannot serve, I nominate **[Alternate Pet Caretaker Name]**.

## Article IX: Business Interests (if applicable)
For any ownership interest in a business or professional practice, I authorize the Executor to continue, sell, or dissolve the interest as needed. Specific succession instructions may be set out in a separate agreement or letter of instruction.

## Article X: Organ Donation and Funeral Wishes
- **Organ Donation:** [Yes/No; specify organs or general authorization if desired].
- **Funeral/Memorial:** [Burial/cremation/green burial]; preferred service details, location, and any cultural or religious preferences.

These wishes are advisory and not intended to create a legal obligation on the Executor or family.

## Article XI: No-Contest Clause
If any beneficiary contests this Will without probable cause, their share shall be reduced to the minimum amount required by law or, if permissible, forfeited.

## Article XII: Governing Law
This Will is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction where I am domiciled at my death, unless a different jurisdiction is expressly stated for a particular asset.
