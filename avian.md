# Avian

Birds are architects of air, turning hollow bones and feathers into a living framework for flight. Their migrations trace maps
overwritten with each season, translating wind and light into guidance systems older than roads.

## Patterns in flight
- **Coordination.** Flocks wheel as one body because each bird watches only a handful of neighbors, proving that complex order
  can emerge from small circles of attention.
- **Adaptation.** From hummingbird hover to swift dives, wing shapes evolve to match precise needs, reminding us that design is
  a conversation with environment.
- **Soundscapes.** Calls and songs stitch together territories and families, turning airwaves into neighborhoods.

## Lessons to borrow
- Protect corridors for movement the way rivers protect water; migration needs safe stops, not just open sky.
- Notice how plumage shifts with seasons, a reminder that appearances can be practical signals, not decoration alone.
- Remember that a perch, a puddle, or a patch of tall grass can be infrastructure for resilience.
