# Porous Matcha Pudding, Milky Way

A spoonable dessert that feels light as foam yet tastes earthy and sweet. The goal is a pudding with tiny air pockets, like a soft sponge, finished with a starry milk syrup.

## Core Idea
- **Porosity** comes from whipping the base before it sets.
- **Matcha clarity** comes from sifting and blooming the powder in warm milk.
- **Milky Way finish** comes from a drizzle of vanilla milk syrup with shimmering sugar crystals.

## Ingredients
### Pudding
- 300 ml whole milk or barista oat milk
- 2 teaspoons matcha, sifted
- 25 g sugar
- 8 g gelatin (or 2 teaspoons agar for a firmer bite)
- 100 ml heavy cream, chilled
- Pinch of salt

### Milky Way Syrup
- 80 ml milk
- 1 tablespoon sweetened condensed milk
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon coarse sugar or edible glitter (optional)

## Method
1. **Bloom**: Sprinkle gelatin over 2 tablespoons cold milk; let it soften for 5 minutes.
2. **Warm base**: Heat remaining milk with sugar and salt to steaming (not boiling). Whisk in matcha until smooth.
3. **Melt**: Stir in bloomed gelatin until dissolved. Let mixture cool to room temperature.
4. **Whip**: Whip cream to soft peaks, then fold into the cooled matcha base. This traps air for the porous texture.
5. **Set**: Pour into glasses and chill 2–3 hours until softly set.

## Milky Way Finish
1. Warm milk and condensed milk just enough to blend.
2. Stir in vanilla and coarse sugar. The crystals mimic tiny stars.
3. Drizzle over the pudding right before serving.

## Serving Notes
- Add a pinch of flaky salt or toasted rice for contrast.
- For extra “galaxy,” dust with matcha and a few sugar pearls.
- Serve cold to keep the airy texture intact.
