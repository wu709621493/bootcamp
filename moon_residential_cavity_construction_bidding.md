# Moon Residential Cavity Construction Bidding

## 1) Project Intent
Build a safe, scalable, and economically repeatable **subsurface lunar residential cavity** suitable for long-duration habitation. The cavity shall prioritize radiation shielding, thermal stability, dust isolation, modular expansion, and emergency compartmentalization.

## 2) Scope of Bid
The awarded contractor (or consortium) will deliver:

- Site survey and geotechnical characterization.
- Excavation or lava-tube adaptation to produce a pressurizable cavity.
- Structural reinforcement and pressure-retention envelope.
- Airlock, utilities trunk, and sealed logistics interface.
- Internal zoning for habitation, life-support, storage, and refuge.
- Commissioning, pressure certification, and handover package.

Optional additive scope:

- Greenhouse annex shell.
- Regolith 3D-printed interior partitions.
- Expanded docking and rover garage module.

## 3) Baseline Technical Requirements

### 3.1 Location and Site Conditions
- Target region: near-polar zone (preferred) or equatorial lava-tube region (alternate).
- Terrain slope at access interface: <= 12 degrees.
- Regolith bearing and fracture characteristics must be documented by in-situ test suite.

### 3.2 Shielding and Structural Safety
- Equivalent shielding target: >= 2.5 m regolith cover or demonstrated equivalent attenuation.
- Pressurized volume design factor: >= 2.0 against burst/failure modes.
- Multi-zone isolation: minimum 3 pressure-isolated compartments.

### 3.3 Habitability Envelope
- Nominal internal pressure: 55-101 kPa (mission profile selectable).
- Temperature range: 18-27 C, controllable by zone.
- Dust ingress at primary lock cycle: <= project-defined particulate threshold.

### 3.4 Reliability and Redundancy
- Critical life-support interfaces: N+1 redundancy minimum.
- Leak detection localization time: <= 120 seconds to compartment level.
- Emergency shelter endurance: >= 120 hours independent support.

## 4) Commercial Bid Package Requirements
Each bidder must submit:

1. **Technical approach** (design narrative + construction method statement).
2. **Integrated schedule** with lunar day/night operational logic.
3. **Cost model** separated into:
   - Earth-based manufacturing,
   - Launch and transit logistics,
   - Lunar surface operations,
   - Commissioning and acceptance.
4. **Mass budget** and launch manifest assumptions.
5. **Power and thermal budget** by construction phase.
6. **Risk register** with quantified probability-impact matrix.
7. **Workforce model** (autonomous robotics vs crewed interventions).
8. **Interface control summary** for third-party life-support systems.

## 5) Bid Evaluation Criteria (100 points)
- Technical compliance and safety margin: **30**
- Total cost of ownership (10-year horizon): **20**
- Schedule credibility and critical-path robustness: **15**
- ISRU utilization level and logistics efficiency: **15**
- Maintainability and upgrade path: **10**
- Team capability and relevant heritage: **10**

Minimum gate conditions:
- Safety score below 70% in technical section: disqualifying.
- Missing pressure certification path: disqualifying.

## 6) Proposed Milestone Framework
- **M0**: Contract award and preliminary design kickoff.
- **M1**: Site data package acceptance.
- **M2**: Preliminary design review (PDR).
- **M3**: Critical design review (CDR).
- **M4**: Earth-based integrated systems test.
- **M5**: Lunar deployment readiness review.
- **M6**: Cavity completion and pressure test.
- **M7**: Operational acceptance and habitation handover.

## 7) Commercial Terms (Draft)
- Contract type: milestone-based hybrid (fixed-price core + incentive options).
- Performance retention: 10% until post-commission reliability demonstration.
- Bonus band: early completion with no loss in safety/reliability KPIs.
- Liquidated damages: schedule slippage beyond approved force majeure.

## 8) Compliance and Governance
- All operations must conform to applicable space treaty obligations and launch-state regulations.
- Planetary protection and contamination controls must be explicitly addressed.
- Data rights and cyber/telemetry integrity requirements must be stated in the proposal.

## 9) Deliverables at Handover
- As-built cavity and interface models.
- Verification and validation dossier.
- Pressure/leak certification reports.
- Maintenance plan, spare list, and training package.
- Incident response playbooks and recovery procedures.

## 10) One-Page Bid Summary Template (Required)
Bidders should include:
- Offered solution name and architecture type.
- Total bid value and major cost drivers.
- Time to initial occupancy.
- Key risks and mitigations.
- Assumed launch cadence and dependency list.
