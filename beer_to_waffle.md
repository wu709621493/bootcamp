# Beer to Waffle

"Beer to waffle" can be read as a playful kitchen transformation: using beer as the liquid in waffle batter.

## Why it works

- **Carbonation** helps aerate batter for a lighter texture.
- **Malt sweetness** adds depth and mild caramel notes.
- **Bitterness** (depending on beer style) can balance syrup or fruit toppings.

## Quick formula

For a standard waffle batter, replace part or all of the milk with beer:

- 2 cups flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 3/4 cups beer (lager or wheat beer for a mild profile)
- 1/3 cup melted butter or neutral oil

Whisk dry and wet separately, combine gently, rest 5-10 minutes, then cook in a preheated waffle iron.

## Style pairing ideas

- **Light lager** -> crisp, neutral waffles.
- **Wheat beer** -> soft, bread-like aroma.
- **Amber ale** -> toastier, nuttier finish.
- **Stout** -> cocoa-like depth (best with chocolate or berries).
