# Propose a Lady

To propose a lady is to propose a promise,
not a pedestal or a polished script.
It is an invitation to walk side by side,
in weather and in wonder,
with the steady courage to keep choosing each other.

I would say:

You are the calm I return to after loud days,
and the spark that makes the quiet glow.
I admire your clarity, your wit, your kindness—
how you meet the world with both softness and strength.

If you are willing,
let us build a life with honest words,
shared mornings, and gentle truths,
where we celebrate the small victories
and learn the long lessons together.

Will you let me stand beside you,
not above or ahead,
but with you—
and take this step, with care and joy?
