# Transparent Cushioning for Donut Channel

## Concept
Transparent cushioning for a donut-shaped channel is a soft, optically clear layer that protects flow surfaces while preserving visibility for monitoring, inspection, or aesthetic design.

## Why use it
- **Impact absorption:** Dampens shock and vibration along curved channel walls.
- **Visual access:** Keeps the channel readable for fluid level, bubbles, or contamination checks.
- **Seal support:** Adds compliant contact surfaces around circular interfaces.
- **Wear reduction:** Helps minimize abrasion at high-contact points.

## Candidate materials
- **Silicone gel/elastomer (clear):** Excellent flexibility and biocompatibility.
- **TPU (transparent grades):** Good abrasion resistance and toughness.
- **PU clear foam/film:** Useful where lightweight cushioning is preferred.
- **Hydrogel composites:** Appropriate for wet-contact or low-friction use cases.

## Design notes
1. Match cushion hardness (Shore scale) to expected load and deformation.
2. Keep optical haze low if machine vision is required.
3. Use anti-yellowing additives for UV-exposed environments.
4. Design drainage/vent paths so trapped air does not distort flow.
5. Validate chemical compatibility with channel media (solvents, oils, detergents).

## Basic validation checklist
- Compression set after repeated loading cycles.
- Transparency retention after UV and thermal aging.
- Leak and seal integrity at pressure extremes.
- Surface scratch resistance and cleanability.
