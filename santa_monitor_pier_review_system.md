# Santa Monitor Pier Review System

## Overview
Santa Monitor is a lightweight review and monitoring system for pier safety, maintenance, and visitor experience. It combines scheduled inspections, sensor data, and public feedback into a single dashboard so operators can prioritize repairs, track compliance, and keep the pier open safely year-round.

## Goals
- Maintain structural integrity and safety compliance.
- Detect hazards early (weather damage, corrosion, crowding, and debris).
- Provide transparent status updates to the public and local agencies.
- Reduce downtime through prioritized maintenance workflows.

## Core Components
1. **Inspection cadence**
   - Weekly visual walk-throughs by staff.
   - Monthly structural checklists by certified inspectors.
   - Post-storm rapid assessments within 24 hours.
2. **Sensor layer**
   - Load sensors at high-traffic spans.
   - Saltwater corrosion probes on pilings.
   - Wind, wave, and tide gauges integrated from local stations.
3. **Public reporting**
   - QR codes on-site linking to a quick hazard report form.
   - Photo uploads with geotagged pier zones.
   - Anonymous option to increase participation.
4. **Review workflow**
   - Automated triage: safety-critical issues auto-escalate.
   - Work orders created with target resolution times.
   - Closure requires photo evidence and inspector sign-off.

## Review Criteria
- **Safety**: structural damage, loose boards, missing railings.
- **Access**: ADA compliance, lighting, signage, and crowd flow.
- **Environmental**: spill risks, waste management, wildlife impacts.
- **Experience**: cleanliness, amenities, and wait times.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Pier operations**: daily checks, incident logging, and signage updates.
- **Engineering**: monthly inspections, remediation plans, and code compliance.
- **City liaison**: public communications, permits, and transparency reporting.

## Data & Reporting
- Unified dashboard with zone-level status (green/yellow/red).
- KPI trends: average time to resolution, incident rate per 1,000 visitors.
- Quarterly public report summarizing repairs, closures, and improvements.

## Governance & Audit
- Maintain immutable logs for inspection outcomes and work orders.
- Annual third-party review of structural assessment practices.
- Data retention policy: 5 years for inspections, 2 years for public reports.

## Implementation Phases
1. **Pilot (0–3 months)**: digitize inspections, create zone map, deploy QR codes.
2. **Expansion (3–9 months)**: add sensors, automate alerts, integrate weather data.
3. **Optimization (9–18 months)**: predictive maintenance, budget forecasting, and public API.

## Success Metrics
- 30% reduction in unplanned closures within 12 months.
- 95% of safety issues addressed within target SLA.
- 20% increase in public hazard reporting participation.
