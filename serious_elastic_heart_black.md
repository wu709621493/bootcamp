# Serious elastic heart black

A serious heart keeps its shape when the world stretches it thin,
elastic in silence, returning to center after every pull.
Black, not as absence, but as a patient field
where light rests and gathers its nerve.

It beats like a metronome in a dim room,
steady against the bright, restless glare.
Each pulse is a small refusal to fray,
a quiet resilience, soft and unbroken.
