# The Fastest Commonwealth Pirate Ship, *The Coconut*

## Dramatis Personae
- **Captain Mara Quill** — Commander of *The Coconut*, sharp-minded and faster than rumor.
- **Master Navigator Ewan Pike** — Keeper of maps, tides, and difficult truths.
- **Quartermaster Sefu Grant** — Former dock worker turned strategist.
- **Governor Elias Wren** — Colonial official, practical but politically trapped.
- **Lieutenant Anne Bell** — Naval officer sent to hunt pirates, conflicted by what she sees.
- **Dockworkers, Sailors, Messengers, Townspeople**

## Historical Note
Set in the early 18th century, this play imagines a pirate vessel called *The Coconut* moving through ports across the Commonwealth seas. While the ship and characters are fictional, the politics of trade monopolies, naval enforcement, and maritime labor draw from real tensions of the age.

## Act I — The Port of Ledgers

**Scene: A crowded colonial dockyard at dawn. Crates stamped with imperial seals. Bells ring.**

**GOVERNOR WREN:**
Every barrel counted, every hand taxed,
and still the grain rots before it reaches inland.

**SEFU GRANT:**
You can count barrels, Governor,
but not hunger.

**WREN:**
I can count cannons. And the Admiralty asks why pirate raids increase.

*(A messenger runs in, soaked.)*

**MESSENGER:**
Another convoy vanished off Cape Mercy.
Witnesses saw a black-and-gold hull,
lateen rig trimmed like a knife.

**WREN:**
Name it.

**MESSENGER:**
*The Coconut*, sir.
Fast as storm-shadow.

*(Murmurs spread.)*

**SEFU:**
They say she never fires first.
Boards, seizes, redistributes,
then leaves coins nailed to the mast.

**WREN:**
Romantic nonsense.
Piracy is theft with better songs.

*(Lights dim. Transition to open sea.)*

---

## Act II — Deck of *The Coconut*

**Scene: Night. Moonlight on taut rigging. The ship cuts through water with uncanny speed.**

**CAPTAIN MARA QUILL:**
Trim to quarter-wind. No lanterns.
We pass under their patrol net before dawn.

**EWAN PIKE:**
You gamble on a current no chart confirms.

**MARA:**
Then the chart learns from us.

**SEFU:**
Cargo list from the captured brig:
flour, medicine, wool bolts, and five chests of silver levies.

**MARA:**
Medicine to Saint Ives Bay.
Flour to the northern settlements.
Silver?

**SEFU:**
Back to the people who paid it.
Quietly.

*(A lookout whistles.)*

**LOOKOUT:**
Sails to starboard! Naval cutter closing!

**MARA:**
No broadside. We outrun.
All hands—remember who taught us speed:
shipwrights unpaid, fishers displaced, rowers whipped.
Their labor built this empire.
Tonight, it builds our escape.

*(Drums mimic heartbeat as ropes fly, sails snap, and the ship surges ahead.)*

---

## Act III — The Pursuit

**Scene: Deck of the naval cutter *Resolute*. Wind rising.**

**LIEUTENANT ANNE BELL:**
Range narrowing. She should not move this quickly.

**HELMSMAN:**
She rides crosswind like she was born to it.

**ANNE:**
Signal warning shot.

*(Cannon boom in distance. No hit.)*

**ANNE (to herself):**
They call them criminals,
yet our convoys escort silk over seed grain.
Law can be blind,
or merely looking elsewhere.

*(A captured sailor from a prior raid is brought forward.)*

**SAILOR:**
She fed us before setting us ashore.
Said, “Tell your captains the sea remembers.”

**ANNE:**
The sea remembers nothing.

**SAILOR:**
Then why are we the ones who keep repeating?

*(Silence. Rain begins.)*

---

## Act IV — Council House and Crossroads

**Scene: Governor’s council chamber. Maps, debt books, and casualty lists.**

**WREN:**
Each report humiliates us.
Merchants demand harsher patrols.
Farm districts demand bread.
I am asked to satisfy both with one empty treasury.

**SEFU (entering under guard):**
Then stop guarding silver harder than children.

**WREN:**
You dare preach after aiding pirates?

**SEFU:**
I aided neighbors.
Your chartered companies buy low, sell high,
and call starvation “market weather.”

**WREN:**
And piracy becomes policy?

**SEFU:**
No. But policy created piracy.

*(Anne enters abruptly.)*

**ANNE:**
Governor, I request terms.
Offer amnesty for surrender of arms,
reprisal hearings for seized wages,
and open port grain reserves.

**WREN:**
You negotiate with thieves?

**ANNE:**
I negotiate with reality.
You cannot cannon a famine into order.

---

## Act V — The Reef of Decision

**Scene: Dawn at a coral reef channel. *The Coconut* cornered by two naval ships.**

**MARA:**
We can run the reef. Fifty-fifty the keel survives.

**EWAN:**
Or accept parley and risk the rope.

*(A signal flag rises from Anne’s ship.)*

**SEFU (reading):**
“Amnesty offered. Grain release guaranteed. Lay down arms and testify.”

**MARA:**
Words are cheaper than gunpowder.

**SEFU:**
True. But if we refuse,
we become the story they already printed.

*(Long pause. Mara studies the horizon.)*

**MARA:**
Strike battle colors.
Raise white and black together.
Not surrender. Witness.

*(They lower weapons. Naval ships hold fire.)*

**ANNE (across the water):**
Captain Quill,
come aboard and speak before the court.

**MARA:**
Only if the dockworkers speak first.

**WREN (from barge, bitterly):**
This is not how empires bargain.

**SEFU:**
Then perhaps this is how they end,
or how they begin again.

*(Final tableau: sailors, officers, laborers facing one another as light rises over calm water.)*

**CURTAIN**
