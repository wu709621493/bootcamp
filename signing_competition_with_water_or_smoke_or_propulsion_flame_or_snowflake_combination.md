# Signing competition with water, smoke, propulsion flame, or snowflake combinations

A signing competition can be framed as a design-and-performance event where each participant creates a symbolic signature from one or more dynamic elements:

- **Water**: flowing strokes, ripples, reflection-driven motifs.
- **Smoke**: diffusion, soft gradients, transient shape language.
- **Propulsion flame**: thrust vectors, ignition rhythm, energetic contrast.
- **Snowflake**: symmetry, branching geometry, crystalline precision.

## Suggested competition format

1. **Theme draw**: entrants randomly draw one required element.
2. **Combination round**: entrants may combine 2–4 elements into one signature concept.
3. **Execution**: live demonstration (analog, digital, or mixed media).
4. **Judging criteria**:
   - Originality of combined motif
   - Visual coherence
   - Motion or transformation storytelling
   - Technical craft and safety handling

## Sample concept directions

- **Water + Smoke**: soft calligraphy that evaporates into mist.
- **Flame + Snowflake**: thermal contrast signature with radial symmetry and directional thrust.
- **Water + Flame + Snowflake**: phase-transition narrative from ice to steam to propulsion.

## Notes on safety and practicality

- Use simulations or controlled effects for flame/smoke demonstrations.
- Prioritize audience visibility and ventilation.
- Keep a reproducible digital version of each submitted signature for archival judging.
