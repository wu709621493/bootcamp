# Kongdong Pi

Kongdong Pi (崆峒僻) refers to an imagined valley settlement tucked between the sheer cliffs of Mount Kongdong, blending Taoist legends with practical frontier life. The name combines the mystical aura of the mountain with the quiet resilience of people choosing to live at the margins.

## Setting
- **Geography:** Terraced stone paths skirt hanging pines and hidden springs; morning fog makes the upper village feel like a drifting island.
- **Economy:** Residents weave hemp ropes, carve wooden talismans for passing pilgrims, and trade medicinal herbs gathered from shaded gullies.
- **Culture:** Daily life mixes mountain survival with temple festivals—cloud-watching contests, flute duets that echo off the cliffs, and night markets lit by fireflies caught in ceramic lamps.

## Characters
- **The Rope-Maker:** Measures time by how quickly dew dries on hemp fibers; never leaves a knot untied.
- **The Archivist Monk:** Keeps weather logs and dream journals on bamboo slips, claiming both predict landslides.
- **The Courier:** Runs messages along cliffside steps, trusting ravens more than radios.

## Themes
- **Solitude vs. Connection:** Isolation is chosen, not imposed; travelers arrive seeking silence, yet stay for the communal tea on foggy mornings.
- **Myth and Material:** Legends of hidden swords and immortals coexist with mundane tasks—repairing roofs, drying mushrooms, boiling dye.
- **Vertical Time:** Living on cliffs makes every path a timeline; descent recalls the past, ascent imagines the future.

## Vignette
> A bell made from river-stone rings only when wind tunnels through a split pine. Children count the echoes; elders note the pitch. The bell sounds different each season, so the village calendar follows resonance rather than dates.

## Staying in Kongdong Pi
Visitors are welcomed if they can carry their own water and leave with lighter hearts than their packs. The only rule: add one story to the communal ledger before descending the mountain.
