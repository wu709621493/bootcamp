# Bad Apple

"Bad Apple" can refer to several ideas, and context matters:

1. **The proverb** — "One bad apple spoils the bunch," meaning harmful behavior can spread through a group if not addressed early.
2. **The song/video meme** — the well-known monochrome animation associated with Touhou fandom culture, often recreated on unusual devices.
3. **A character judgment** — calling someone a "bad apple" to describe repeated choices that hurt trust, safety, or collaboration.

## Practical takeaway

A better response than labeling is to evaluate behavior patterns and incentives:

- What exactly happened?
- Is it a one-time mistake or a repeated pattern?
- What guardrails prevent recurrence?
- What repair is owed to affected people?

In teams, systems usually matter as much as individuals. Accountability is strongest when paired with clear expectations, transparent consequences, and a path to improvement.
