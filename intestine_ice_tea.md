# Intestine Ice Tea: A Gut-Friendly Cooler

Intestine ice tea is a chilled herbal blend designed to be gentle on digestion. It focuses on mild botanicals, a touch of sweetness, and cooling aromatics so the drink feels refreshing without being harsh or overly acidic.

## Flavor Profile
- **Softly herbal** with chamomile or rooibos as a base.
- **Cooling notes** from mint or fennel.
- **Light sweetness** from honey or a splash of apple juice.
- **Bright lift** from a thin strip of citrus zest, not juice.

## Ingredients (4 servings)
- 4 cups water
- 3 chamomile tea bags (or 2 tablespoons loose-leaf)
- 1 teaspoon crushed fennel seed
- 4 fresh mint sprigs
- 1 to 2 teaspoons honey, to taste
- 2 thin strips of lemon or orange zest
- Ice for serving

## Method
1. Bring the water just to a simmer, then turn off the heat.
2. Add chamomile, fennel, mint, and citrus zest. Cover and steep for 6 to 8 minutes.
3. Strain the tea, then stir in honey while warm.
4. Chill for at least 1 hour. Serve over ice.

## Gut-Friendly Tips
- Keep the steep time short so tannins stay low and the tea remains mellow.
- Use zest rather than juice to avoid extra acidity.
- Serve cold, not icy-cold, if you want the gentlest sip.

## Variations
- **Ginger-mint:** Add a 1-inch slice of fresh ginger for a warming finish.
- **Vanilla-calming:** Add a few drops of vanilla extract after chilling.
- **No-caffeine:** Keep the base herbal or use decaf green tea.

## Serving Notes
Pair with simple foods like toast, rice bowls, or fruit. It is meant to be calming and light, not a replacement for meals or medical care.
