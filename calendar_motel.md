# Calendar Motel

A **calendar motel** is a metaphor for a place in your planning system where tasks can stay for a limited time—long enough to be handled, but not long enough to become forgotten clutter.

## Core idea

Like guests in a motel, commitments should:

1. **Check in** with a clear date and purpose.
2. **Stay briefly** while active.
3. **Check out** when completed, delegated, or canceled.

## Why use it

- Prevents your calendar from becoming a permanent storage attic.
- Surfaces stale commitments that need a decision.
- Keeps daily planning lightweight and realistic.

## Simple workflow

1. Add only date-bound or near-term items to the calendar motel.
2. During weekly review, scan for overstay items.
3. For each overstay item, do one action: finish, move to a task list, or drop.
4. Keep future months mostly open except for true fixed events.

## Overstay rule (example)

If an item has been moved more than **twice** without progress, it must be rewritten into a smaller next action or removed.

## Practical examples

- “Prepare slides” stays for two focused blocks this week; unfinished parts move to project tasks.
- “Read paper backlog” does not live on the calendar; it belongs in a reading queue.
- “Doctor appointment” is a fixed guest and keeps its room.

A calendar motel helps your schedule remain a tool for execution, not a museum of postponed intentions.
