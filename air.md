# Air

Air is the common medium for breath, sound, and scent; an invisible infrastructure that stitches together living things and the weather they inhabit.

## Properties
- **Elastic memory.** Air yields to movement but springs back, carrying echoes and turbulence that reveal what passed through.
- **Carrier of change.** Temperature, spores, and signals ride its currents, making air the first messenger of approaching seasons and storms.
- **Shared resource.** Every lungful is borrowed from a communal reservoir, reminding us that clean air is both a right and a responsibility.

## Practices
- Open windows when the day is quiet to let indoor air trade places with the world outside.
- Plant and protect trees; their leaves are quiet filters that return spent air as a fresh invitation to breathe.
- Notice the wind's direction before speaking loudly outdoors—sound travels differently with or against the flow.

Air asks for stewardship even though it cannot be held. In caring for it, we care for ourselves and for strangers we will never meet.
