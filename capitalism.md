# Capitalism

Capitalism is an economic system where most production and exchange are privately owned and coordinated through markets.

## Core ideas

- **Private property:** Individuals and firms own assets and businesses.
- **Market exchange:** Prices are shaped by supply and demand.
- **Profit motive:** Producers seek profit, which can drive investment and innovation.
- **Competition:** Rival firms compete for customers, often improving quality and lowering costs.

## Strengths often cited

- Encourages entrepreneurship and innovation.
- Can allocate resources efficiently through price signals.
- Offers consumers broad choice.

## Common criticisms

- Can produce inequality in wealth and income.
- May underprovide public goods without policy intervention.
- Can incentivize short-term profit over long-term social or environmental outcomes.

In practice, most countries use **mixed economies** that blend capitalist markets with government regulation and social programs.
