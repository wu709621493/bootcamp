# Forest Penetration Rate

"Forest penetration rate" is most commonly used to mean **forest coverage rate**:

\[
\text{Forest penetration rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Forest area}}{\text{Total land area}} \times 100
\]

## Example
If a country has 42 million hectares of forest and 120 million hectares of total land area:

\[
\frac{42}{120} \times 100 = 35\%
\]

So the forest penetration rate is **35%**.

> Note: In policy and statistical reports, the term is often written as **forest coverage**, **forest cover rate**, or **forest area share**.
