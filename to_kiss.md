# To Kiss

To kiss is to pause the clock and let breath become language.
It is the brief treaty where skin signs its name in warmth.
It is a question answered by leaning forward instead of speaking.
It is the smallest journey that still requires trust in its distance.
It is a lantern lit between two travelers, bright enough to remember the path back.
It is consented closeness, soft but decisive, saying, "we are here, together, now."
