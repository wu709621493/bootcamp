# Male Duck Bowl Design

## Overview
A functional serving bowl inspired by a male duck (drake), balancing whimsical form with everyday usability. The bowl reads as a resting drake from above, with the interior optimized for soup, rice, or snack service while the exterior captures the signature colors and silhouette.

## Design Goals
- Celebrate the drake profile with recognizable head, bill, and chest shapes.
- Keep the interior volume smooth and easy to clean.
- Provide stable footing so the piece does not tip when filled.
- Use food-safe materials and finishes suitable for hot and cold contents.

## Form & Silhouette
- **Top view**: Oval body with a subtle notch suggesting a wing fold on the right side.
- **Head & bill**: A compact protrusion forming a pouring lip; the bill acts as a small spout.
- **Tail**: Raised, curled tail feathers function as a grip point for lifting.

## Dimensions (Nominal)
- **Overall length**: 260 mm
- **Overall width**: 180 mm
- **Overall height**: 95 mm
- **Interior depth**: 55 mm
- **Capacity**: 1.1–1.3 L

## Interior Geometry
- Gentle, continuous radius from base to wall for easy stirring and cleaning.
- Flat center pad (80 mm diameter) for stability when the bowl is set down.
- 5-degree inward taper to reduce slosh and keep contents centered.

## Exterior Details
- **Head**: Slightly raised crown with a carved eye recess (no sharp edges).
- **Neck**: Subtle ridge line marking the classic drake collar band.
- **Wing panel**: Shallow relief feather pattern, kept low to avoid dirt traps.
- **Tail**: Three upward flicks, each rounded for safe handling.

## Materials & Finish
- **Primary material**: High-fire stoneware or porcelain.
- **Glaze system**:
  - Head: Deep emerald or bottle green.
  - Collar band: Thin white ring.
  - Body: Warm chestnut brown.
  - Wing panel: Iridescent blue-black accent.
  - Bill: Satin mustard glaze with slight matte texture.
- **Food safety**: Lead-free, cadmium-free certified glazes.

## Color Palette
- Emerald green (#0B5B3C)
- Chestnut brown (#6B3B1E)
- Wing blue-black (#1B2633)
- Collar white (#F2F2F2)
- Bill mustard (#D2A23A)

## Ergonomics & Use
- Tail grip supports one-handed pour from the bill spout.
- Rim thickness of 6–7 mm provides comfort and chip resistance.
- Base ring (110 mm diameter) for stable placement on tables.

## Manufacturing Notes
- Designed for slip casting or jiggering with a two-part mold.
- Add a vent hole under the tail to prevent air trapping during casting.
- Foot ring should be trimmed after bisque firing for clean finish.

## Packaging & Care
- Pack with a molded pulp cradle that protects the head and tail.
- Dishwasher safe; avoid thermal shock (no direct flame).

## Optional Variants

- **Serving platter**: Flattened drake body, 340 mm length.
- **Limited edition**: Gold lustre on collar band and wing panel.
