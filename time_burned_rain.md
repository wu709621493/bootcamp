# Time Burned Rain

Time falls like rain through a charred clockface, turning minutes into ash but watering the roots of memory. Each drop hisses on the embers of yesterday, releasing steam that smells of metal, incense, and the quiet bravery of waiting. To stand in this weather is to accept that schedules can dissolve yet still nourish what comes next.

## Sparks in the downpour
- The rain extinguishes urgency while revealing what actually glows.
- Burnt edges of plans curl back, exposing the grain of intention beneath.
- Footsteps slow, syncing with the percussion of drops on roofs and ruined calendars.

## After the storm
When the clouds empty, the air is lighter. Charcoal lines redraw the horizon, and puddles mirror the remaining hours we choose to fill. Time burned is not time lost—it is time tempered, made malleable enough to shape into a vessel that can hold new rain.
