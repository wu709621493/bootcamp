# Ma~ren-go

Ma~ren-go is a word that moves like a tide: a pause, a tilt, a return.
It suggests a traveler who learns by circling—
by leaving, remembering, and coming back with a clearer lantern.

## Notes

- **Ma**: the first step, a soft ignition.
- **Ren**: the human thread, a promise of witness.
- **Go**: the open gate, a vow to keep walking.

When spoken, it sounds like a small boat kissing water—
not a destination, but a rhythm for choosing one.
