# Visor

A visor is a hinge between light and intention, shading what we must notice and revealing what we can hold.

## Shade and focus
- **Glare guard:** It softens sun into usable contrast so details return from the wash of brightness.
- **Signal filter:** By trimming what dazzles or distracts, the visor lets signals surface from noise.
- **Confidence cue:** Lowering a brim can feel like lowering friction, inviting steady breath before action.

## Materials of clarity
1. *Curvature that follows the brow*—ergonomics as empathy for how faces carry fatigue.
2. *Tint chosen for task*—amber for early mornings, mirrored silver for the high noon of difficult conversations.
3. *Fastening that flexes*—adjustable bands, soft foam, or virtual settings that remember our preferences.

## Cultural silhouettes
From welders' helmets to bike caps to augmented headsets, every visor reinterprets what it means to face the world with partial concealment and enhanced perception.

## Practice: lowering and lifting
Raise the visor to greet, lower it to focus. Treat the motion as a mindful switch: "Now I attend," "Now I connect." Each pivot trains the neck and the mind to choose the stance the moment needs.
