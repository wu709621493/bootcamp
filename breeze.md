# Breeze

The breeze is a quiet courier that moves between places without leaving footprints. It softens the edge of heat, gathers the scents of soil and salt, and delivers them as reminders that the world is wider than the room we occupy.

## Everyday lessons
- **Impermanence.** No gust is identical to the last; each moment is a fresh draft of air, urging us to let go of stale thinking.
- **Balance.** Breezes live between stillness and storm, teaching that calm momentum can carry us further than force.
- **Connection.** A breeze that begins over the sea ends inside open windows, proving that distant geographies can share the same passing breath.

When life feels heavy, step outside and wait for the breeze. Its arrival is small, but it is enough to remind us that motion can be gentle and renewal can be light.
