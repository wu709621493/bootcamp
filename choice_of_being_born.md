# Choice of Being Born

No one signs the first contract.

We arrive without consent,
carried by histories,
by biology,
by chance,
by other people’s hopes and unfinished stories.

So the first choice is never whether to be born.
The first choice is what to do **after** we notice we are here.

To inherit pain and still refuse cruelty.
To inherit silence and still learn language.
To inherit fear and still practice love.

Maybe freedom is not the power to choose the beginning,
but the courage to author the middle.

And if meaning is not given,
then every gentle act is a vote:

I did not choose to enter,
but I choose, today,
how I will stay.
