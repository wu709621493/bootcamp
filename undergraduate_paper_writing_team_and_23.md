# Undergraduate Paper Writing Team and 23-Week Execution Plan

## Objective
Build a student-led paper writing team that can produce one submission-ready undergraduate research paper within 23 weeks.

## Team Structure
- **Team lead (1):** Owns timeline, meeting rhythm, and final integration.
- **Research designers (2):** Define the question, hypothesis, and methods.
- **Literature analysts (4):** Review and annotate prior work.
- **Data/experiment operators (4):** Execute collection or implementation.
- **Statistics & validation (3):** Ensure result quality and reproducibility.
- **Writers (6):** Draft each section and maintain style consistency.
- **Visuals & references (2):** Figures, tables, and citation management.
- **Submission coordinator (1):** Formatting, checklist, and upload process.

Total: **23 members**.

## 23-Week Timeline
1. Team onboarding, role assignment, and communication protocol.
2. Topic screening and feasibility check.
3. Finalize research question and scope.
4. Build shared bibliography and reading matrix.
5. Literature synthesis and gap statement.
6. Methods draft v1.
7. Pilot test or small data dry run.
8. Refine methods based on pilot.
9. Full data collection / implementation starts.
10. Progress audit and quality control pass.
11. Continue execution; resolve blockers.
12. Midpoint checkpoint and risk review.
13. Data cleaning and preprocessing.
14. Statistical analysis / model evaluation.
15. Robustness checks and alternative explanations.
16. Build figures and core results tables.
17. Draft Introduction + Related Work.
18. Draft Methods + Results.
19. Draft Discussion + Limitations + Conclusion.
20. Internal peer review round 1.
21. Internal peer review round 2 and language polishing.
22. Formatting to target venue + reference verification.
23. Final sign-off and submission.

## Quality Control Checklist
- A single version-controlled source of truth for manuscript and data.
- Citation style validated before final week.
- Every figure has a caption, source, and interpretation sentence.
- Claims are tied to evidence in text, table, or appendix.
- At least one reproducibility run completed by a non-author teammate.

## Expected Deliverables
- Submission-ready manuscript.
- Supplementary package (data dictionary, codebook, and analysis scripts).
- One-page retrospective on lessons learned for next cohort.
