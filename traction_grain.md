# Traction Grain

Traction grain is the smallest useful unit of grip in a movement system.

It can describe:
- a tire touching rough road,
- a climber's shoe on textured stone,
- a robot wheel crossing dust and debris,
- or even the psychological "bite" that helps a team move from idea to action.

## Practical framing

A simple way to think about traction grain is:

**Traction = surface texture × contact pressure × timing control**

When one term weakens, motion slips. When all three align, motion stabilizes.

## Design takeaway

If you want better traction, don't only add force. Improve the grain:
- shape the surface,
- tune the contact,
- and synchronize the step.
