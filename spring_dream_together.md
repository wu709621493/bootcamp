# Spring Dream Together

Spring arrives like a shared whisper, a soft breeze threading through open windows and open hearts. It carries the scent of waking soil and the delicate confidence of buds unfolding. We meet it together, palms up, ready to receive the season's gentle vow.

In the morning light, our dreams stretch like new leaves, tasting the warmth. We speak in the language of small beginnings: the first cup of tea, the first laugh, the first promise to keep walking side by side. Every step is an invitation to renew what we thought we already knew about each other.

Together, we imagine a garden that remembers our names. We dream of paths lined with kindness, of birds that rehearse the songs we need, of rain that knows when to arrive and when to step away. The dream is not fragile; it is rooted in the way we choose to show up, again and again.

Let spring be our shared rehearsal. Let the blossoms remind us how to open without fear, how to lean toward light without losing the steadiness of our roots. When we dream together, we make a season larger than ourselves.

May this spring hold our hands in its soft green promise. May we keep dreaming, keep tending, and keep growing in the same bright direction.
