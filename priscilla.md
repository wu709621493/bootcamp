# Priscilla

## Snapshot
- Lives with intentional simplicity, preferring essentials over extras.
- Keeps a small circle but invests deeply in each friendship.
- Practices morning walks as a moving meditation and creative recharge.

## Guiding principles
1. **Steadiness over spectacle.** Priscilla moves at a sustainable pace, valuing consistency more than bursts of attention.
2. **Quiet competence.** She solves problems calmly, speaking up only when her words add clarity.
3. **Hospitality of attention.** When she listens, people feel like the room shrinks to just the conversation.

## Current focus
- Hosting a weekly community breakfast that pairs neighbors who rarely cross paths.
- Learning botanical illustration to document native plants in a nearby park.
- Building a personal archive of letters to future selves, reviewing them each season to track growth.

## How she supports others
- Turns vague ideas into first drafts and next steps.
- Remembers commitments and gently follows up so projects do not drift.
- Creates "quiet corners" at gatherings where reflection and slower conversations are welcome.

## What she needs
- Time buffers between commitments to reset her energy.
- Occasional encouragement to share unfinished work before it feels perfect.
- Spaces with natural light and open windows; fresh air keeps her grounded.

## A note for collaborators
If Priscilla pauses before answering, it is because she is lining up her thoughts with care. Inviting that pause tends to bring out her clearest insights.
