# Young Mode

Young Mode is a mental setting for learning and creating with the openness, energy, and adaptability often associated with youth.
It does not depend on age; it is a deliberate posture you can choose when you want to explore, iterate, and grow quickly.

## Core principles

- **Curiosity first:** Lead with questions, not conclusions, and treat every topic as a door worth opening.
- **Low ego, high iteration:** Test ideas fast, learn from feedback, and improve without getting attached to first drafts.
- **Playful rigor:** Be willing to experiment while still respecting facts, methods, and evidence.
- **Generous attention:** Listen widely, seek diverse perspectives, and learn from people at different stages.
- **Healthy limits:** Protect sleep, movement, and nutrition so your mind can stay flexible.

## Practices

1. Keep a "learning backlog" of small questions you can answer in 15 minutes.
2. Use short cycles: plan, try, reflect, and repeat.
3. Share early work with a trusted peer and ask for one clear improvement.
4. Replace "I can’t" with "I can’t yet" to keep momentum.
5. Celebrate small gains to reinforce progress.

## Why it helps

Young Mode lowers friction between curiosity and action.
It helps you stay resilient, keep creativity high, and make consistent forward progress in learning-heavy work.

## When to switch out

Use Young Mode to explore, brainstorm, and prototype.
Switch out when you need deep focus for long execution blocks, or when decisions must be final and well-documented.
