# Song Face

A face remembers every chorus we have sung,
cheeks bright as cymbals catching sunset chords.
Eyes hold the echo of the last refrain,
where harmony softens the edges of worry.

Lips move like vinyl spinning gentle grooves,
a melody traced across freckles and laughter.
Beneath the skin a drumbeat steadies the night,
reminding the heart that silence is also music.

When the song ends and the lights fall closed,
the face remains, humming verses in the dark—
a quiet album of breaths, pauses, and grace,
ready to rise with the morning’s first note.
