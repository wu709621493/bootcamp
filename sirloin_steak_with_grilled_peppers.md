# Sirloin Steak with Grilled Peppers (Medium-Rare)

A simple backyard-friendly steak dinner that pairs juicy sirloin with sweet, lightly charred peppers. This guide keeps the steak medium-rare and the peppers tender-crisp.

## Ingredients (Serves 2)
- 2 sirloin steaks (8–10 oz / 225–280 g each), about 1-inch thick
- 2 bell peppers, seeded and cut into thick strips
- 1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for the peppers
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 cloves garlic, lightly crushed
- 2–3 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Equipment
- Grill or grill pan
- Instant-read thermometer (recommended)
- Tongs

## Preparation
1. **Season the steak:** Pat steaks dry. Season all sides generously with kosher salt and black pepper. Let stand at room temperature for 20–30 minutes while you prep the peppers.
2. **Prep the peppers:** Toss pepper strips with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.

## Cooking
1. **Preheat grill:** Heat to medium-high. Clean and oil the grates.
2. **Grill peppers:** Place peppers over direct heat. Grill 6–8 minutes, turning occasionally, until tender with light char. Move to a cooler zone to keep warm.
3. **Grill steak:** Place steaks over direct heat. Grill 4–5 minutes on the first side until a deep brown crust forms.
4. **Add aromatics:** Flip steaks. Top each with a small pat of butter, a crushed garlic clove, and a herb sprig. Grill another 3–5 minutes to medium-rare (125–130°F / 52–54°C internal). Adjust time for thicker steaks or desired doneness.
5. **Rest:** Transfer steaks to a board, tent loosely with foil, and rest 5–10 minutes. Remove garlic and herbs.

## Serving
- Slice against the grain and spoon over any resting juices.
- Serve alongside the grilled peppers. Add a squeeze of lemon or a drizzle of balsamic if desired.

## Tips for Success
- **Dry surfaces brown better:** Moisture on steak or peppers steams instead of sears—pat them dry before seasoning.
- **Use two heat zones:** If possible, keep one area of the grill cooler to finish cooking gently and to hold peppers warm.
- **Check temperature:** Pull steaks a few degrees below target; they rise slightly during resting.
