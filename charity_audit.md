# Charity Audit

## Scope
This audit reviews the governance model described in [`慈善制.md`](慈善制.md), with focus on transparency, accountability, operational controls, beneficiary protection, and measurable impact.

## Executive Summary
The proposed model is strong on structural intent (multi-level platforms, legal alignment, third-party evaluation), but it needs tighter control definitions to be audit-ready in implementation. The largest gaps are:

1. Missing hard control thresholds (for timeliness, disclosure completeness, and incident response).
2. Limited anti-fraud and conflict-of-interest procedures.
3. No explicit data-retention and privacy-control matrix.
4. Lack of standardized impact metrics that can be compared across regions.

## Control Review

### 1) Governance and Oversight
**Strengths**
- Clear multi-stakeholder design (government, market, social organizations).
- Phased rollout with pilot-to-scale pathway.

**Gaps**
- Board/committee authority boundaries are not explicitly documented.
- No mandatory rotation or independence criteria for evaluators.

**Audit Recommendation**
- Define a governance charter with decision rights, escalation paths, and annual independence declarations.

### 2) Financial Transparency
**Strengths**
- Emphasis on information disclosure and public trust.

**Gaps**
- No minimum disclosure dataset (e.g., donation source class, administrative ratio, restricted-fund usage).
- No disclosure SLA (e.g., transactions posted within T+3 business days).

**Audit Recommendation**
- Publish a required disclosure schema and a compliance dashboard per institution.

### 3) Fraud and Ethics Controls
**Strengths**
- Public supervision is acknowledged as a trust mechanism.

**Gaps**
- No whistleblower channel design.
- No conflict-of-interest register for donors, vendors, and administrators.
- No anomaly detection workflow.

**Audit Recommendation**
- Introduce triage process for reports, protected reporting channels, and quarterly fraud-risk scans.

### 4) Beneficiary Protection
**Strengths**
- Inclusive and universal participation goals.

**Gaps**
- No harm-prevention protocol for vulnerable groups.
- No grievance handling SLA for beneficiaries.

**Audit Recommendation**
- Add beneficiary rights statement, consent standards, and complaint-resolution timelines.

### 5) Data Protection and Compliance
**Strengths**
- Regulatory alignment is explicitly mentioned.

**Gaps**
- No data classification, retention, or deletion standards.
- No role-based access model specified for national/provincial/city/county platforms.

**Audit Recommendation**
- Implement a data control matrix: classification, retention term, legal basis, access owner, and audit log policy.

### 6) Impact Measurement
**Strengths**
- Focus on sustainability and social value.

**Gaps**
- No KPI taxonomy for outcomes vs outputs.
- No baseline/counterfactual guidance.

**Audit Recommendation**
- Require common KPI packs (education, health, emergency relief, elderly care) and independent annual impact verification.

## Suggested Audit KPIs
- **Disclosure timeliness rate**: % records posted within policy SLA.
- **Restricted-fund compliance rate**: % funds used within approved purpose.
- **Fraud incident closure time**: median days from report to resolution.
- **Beneficiary complaint resolution rate**: % cases resolved within SLA.
- **Program outcome conversion**: % beneficiaries reaching target outcomes.
- **Administrative cost ratio**: management/overhead as % of total use of funds.

## Priority Remediation Plan (90 Days)
1. **Days 1–30**: finalize governance charter, conflict-of-interest policy, whistleblower workflow.
2. **Days 31–60**: release disclosure schema and KPI definitions; deploy pilot dashboard.
3. **Days 61–90**: run first control test cycle, publish findings, and issue corrective-action tracker.

## Overall Audit Opinion
**Conditionally adequate design, not yet implementation-ready.**
The framework can support a high-trust charity ecosystem if minimum control standards are codified and independently tested before full-scale rollout.
