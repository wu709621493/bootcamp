# Fitting Room Charity

## Concept
**Fitting Room Charity** is a donation model placed inside clothing stores: shoppers can leave gently used items, accessories, or small essentials in a dedicated fitting-room-adjacent drop zone. The core idea is to turn moments of personal choice ("keep, return, or donate") into immediate community support.

## Why it works
- **Low friction:** The donor is already handling clothes and making decisions.
- **Timely relevance:** Items are sorted where sizing and condition are top of mind.
- **Shared dignity:** Presentation standards can mirror retail quality instead of “discard bins.”

## Basic operating model
1. **Collection point:** Clearly labeled, supervised donation cabinet near fitting rooms.
2. **Fast triage:** Staff/volunteers perform quick checks for cleanliness and usability.
3. **Routing:** Items go to partner shelters, school programs, migrant centers, or resale-for-cause channels.
4. **Transparency:** Monthly reporting on number of items, categories, and recipient organizations.

## Donation standards
- Clean and wearable clothing only.
- No damaged undergarments, unsafe items, or heavily stained fabrics.
- Seasonal tagging (winter coat, school uniform, infant wear) to improve matching.

## Inclusion and privacy
- Donors can contribute anonymously.
- Recipients should access items through respectful channels (appointments, vouchers, community closets).
- Avoid public photos of recipients unless explicit consent is documented.

## Suggested metrics
- Total items collected per month.
- Percentage successfully redistributed.
- Average time from donation to recipient delivery.
- Recipient satisfaction and fit-success rate.
- Landfill diversion estimate (kg textile waste avoided).

## Pilot checklist (first 8 weeks)
- Week 1–2: Select partner nonprofits and define accepted-item policy.
- Week 3–4: Install signage, bins, and staff SOP cards.
- Week 5–6: Start pilot in one store and record baseline metrics.
- Week 7–8: Review data, adjust acceptance criteria, expand if stable.

## One-line mission
**Make generosity as easy as trying on a shirt.**
