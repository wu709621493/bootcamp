# Headset

## Overview
A headset combines headphones and a microphone into a single wearable device for listening and speaking. Modern headsets are used for gaming, video calls, call centers, and mixed reality, prioritizing comfort, clear audio, and reliable connectivity.

## Common Types
- **Wired headsets**: Connect via 3.5 mm, USB-A, or USB-C for low latency and consistent power.
- **Bluetooth headsets**: Provide wireless freedom, often with active noise cancellation and multipoint pairing.
- **Gaming headsets**: Emphasize positional audio, low latency, and boom microphones.
- **Office headsets**: Focus on voice clarity, long-wear comfort, and background-noise suppression.
- **True wireless earbuds**: Compact in-ear designs with separate left/right units and a charging case.

## Core Components
- **Drivers**: Convert electrical signals to sound; dynamic drivers are common, while planar magnetic or balanced armature drivers target higher fidelity.
- **Microphones**: Boom or inline mics with noise suppression improve speech intelligibility.
- **Control electronics**: Handle amplification, ANC, and wireless codecs.
- **Cushions and headband**: Materials and clamping force determine comfort and isolation.
- **Connectivity**: Wired connectors, Bluetooth radios, or USB dongles define compatibility and latency.

## Performance Factors
- **Frequency response**: Indicates tonal balance; a neutral response helps accuracy, while bass-boosted tuning adds impact.
- **Latency**: Critical for gaming and video calls; wired or low-latency codecs (LC3, aptX Adaptive) help.
- **Noise isolation**: Passive sealing plus active noise cancellation reduces ambient noise.
- **Microphone clarity**: Measured by signal-to-noise ratio and pickup pattern; cardioid patterns reduce room noise.
- **Battery life**: Wireless headsets trade size against runtime; fast charging adds convenience.

## Fit and Comfort Tips
- Choose earcup size and padding that distribute pressure evenly.
- Adjust headband height so drivers align with ear canals.
- For long sessions, prioritize breathable materials and lighter weight.

## Care and Maintenance
- Wipe pads regularly to remove oils and extend lifespan.
- Store in a case to protect from dust and impact.
- Replace worn cushions to maintain seal and sound quality.

## Emerging Trends
- **Spatial audio**: Head-tracked rendering for immersive soundscapes.
- **AI noise reduction**: On-device processing to isolate speech in noisy spaces.
- **Hybrid connectivity**: Seamless switching between wired and wireless modes.
