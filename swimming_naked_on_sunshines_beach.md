# Swimming Naked on Sunshine’s Beach

The sea wears only light today, a sheer blue dress it keeps unbuttoned.
We step out of our names, slipping into salt like a second skin.
Sunbeams braid our shoulders; the tide counts our breaths and forgets them.

Sand is a warm language underfoot, syllables of quartz and hush.
Gulls write pale commas in the sky, pausing the sentence of the horizon.
We float in the laughter of water, a bright and honest silence.

Every wave is a door without a lock, opening to a cooler room.
We move through it weightless, as if the body were a rumor.
The world applauds with surf, rinsing away the day’s old dust.

Later, we dry in the wind’s slow pages, reading the sun back into our bones.
Salt crystals bloom like tiny stars; our footprints are erased on purpose.
We leave the beach unclothed in light, and carry it home as proof.
