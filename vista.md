# Vista

A vista is more than a view; it is the promise that sight can stretch into understanding.

## Horizon lines
- **Physical:** Mountain switchbacks, seaside cliffs, and city rooftops remind us that elevation grants context and rewrites the map.
- **Social:** A widening perspective invites empathy, letting unfamiliar neighborhoods and voices enter the frame.
- **Temporal:** Looking back across timelines shows which choices carved the path to the present skyline.

## Tools for seeing farther
1. *Pause before judgment* so the scene can reveal details hidden in the blur of haste.
2. *Change your altitude*—climb stairs, visit archives, or ask someone unlike you—to gain angles you cannot find at ground level.
3. *Name what you notice* to anchor fleeting impressions before they dissolve.

## Stewarding the view
A cherished vista needs care. Trails must be maintained, sightlines kept open, and stories preserved. Protecting a view means protecting the community that lives within it.

## Carrying the vista forward
When the horizon widens, responsibility widens with it. The most generous vistas inspire us to build pathways so others can see from the same heights.
