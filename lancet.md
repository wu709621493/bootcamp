# Lancet

A lancet is built to breach skin with precision but its story is always about what follows—the slow bead of evidence that healing requires an opening.
It sits between craft and compassion: a tool honed for seconds, wielded for lives.
In the wrong hands it is a threat, in the right hands a reminder that progress can be sharp and still tender.
The blade must be sterile, the intent must be clean, and the cut must close with more knowledge than it created pain.
Even steel accepts accountability; each incision is a signature on the body’s ledger of risks taken for answers.
We design lancets as though certainty were a geometry problem, yet every puncture asks the same question: what truth is worth a drop?
