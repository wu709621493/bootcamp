# Follow-me moon

## Overview
Follow-me moon is a gentle navigation idea for nights when people want to move safely without flooding streets with harsh light. The moon does not chase us; it leads by calm reference. The system borrows that idea: soft, layered guidance that “follows” walkers, cyclists, and night workers while keeping the sky dark and the ground readable.

## Core principles
- **Low-glare guidance:** Use warm, shielded lights and reflective cues that preserve night vision.
- **Human-paced movement:** Illumination rises only where people are, then fades behind them.
- **Ecology-aware timing:** Lighting respects migration and nesting windows; activity maps prevent constant glare.
- **Shared wayfinding:** Subtle markers and ground patterns create a steady trail without a bright beam.

## Night flow
1. **Dusk:** Path markers activate at a low baseline, enough to outline edges.
2. **Evening:** Sensors soften illumination as footsteps approach, keeping a moonlike halo.
3. **Late night:** Paths switch to reflective-only guidance where traffic is rare.
4. **Dawn:** Lights taper off to let daylight take over without a hard cutoff.

## Benefits
- Calmer streets with less visual stress.
- Safer navigation for people who prefer softer light.
- Reduced energy use and lower impact on nocturnal wildlife.

## Closing note
Follow-me moon is a reminder that guidance can be gentle: the path follows the person, and the night stays intact.
