# Copy Problem per Answer

"Copy problem per answer" is a simple workflow rule: before writing each answer, restate the exact problem or prompt that the answer addresses.

## Why use it

- Prevents answers from drifting away from the question.
- Makes grading, review, and collaboration easier.
- Creates a clean record when answers are separated, reordered, or quoted later.
- Helps the writer confirm they understood the task before solving it.

## Recommended workflow

1. **Copy the prompt** directly above the answer.
2. **Keep it short but exact**—trim only irrelevant context.
3. **Answer immediately below** the copied problem.
4. **Repeat for every item** instead of answering all questions in one undifferentiated block.

## Example

- **Problem:** Explain why replication matters in an experiment.
- **Answer:** Replication helps distinguish real effects from random variation and improves confidence in the result.

## Practical note

This habit is especially useful in homework sets, lab writeups, interviews, and AI-assisted drafting, where answers can otherwise lose their connection to the original prompt.
