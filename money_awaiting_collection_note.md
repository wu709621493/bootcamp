# Note of Money Awaiting Collection

A **note of money awaiting collection** is a short written reminder that payment is still due.
It is commonly used for invoices, loans, reimbursements, or other unsettled balances.

## Purpose

- Confirm the amount that remains unpaid.
- Remind the payer of the original due date.
- Provide instructions for how to pay.
- Keep communication professional and documented.

## Simple Template

**Subject:** Payment Reminder – Amount Awaiting Collection

Dear [Name],

This is a friendly reminder that **[amount]** is still awaiting collection for **[invoice/reference]**, originally due on **[date]**.

Please complete payment by **[new date]** using **[payment method/details]**.

If you have already paid, please disregard this message and share the payment confirmation.

Thank you.

Sincerely,  
[Your Name]  
[Role/Company]  
[Contact Information]

## Optional Short Version

"Kind reminder: **[amount]** for **[reference]** remains unpaid and is awaiting collection. Please settle by **[date]**."
