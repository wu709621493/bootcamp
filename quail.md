# Quail

Quail carry quiet boldness in a small frame. They move in coveys, keeping low to the ground, reading brush and shadow like a map, and trusting short bursts of flight to escape danger.

## What it teaches
- **Preparedness.** Quail pause and listen, showing how a moment of stillness can reveal the safest path forward.
- **Community.** Covey life turns vulnerability into resilience, a reminder that shared attention keeps everyone safer.
- **Agility.** Their sudden takeoff is all momentum and decisiveness, proof that small bodies can still make decisive moves.

In their rustle through grass and scrub, quail remind us that careful attention and collective rhythm can turn quiet places into refuge.
