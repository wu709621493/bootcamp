# Hygiene

Hygiene is the set of daily habits that keeps the body, food, and environment clean enough to reduce illness and improve well-being.

## Personal hygiene basics

- Wash hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after using the restroom.
- Brush teeth twice a day and floss daily.
- Bathe regularly and keep nails trimmed.
- Cover coughs/sneezes with a tissue or elbow.
- Launder clothes, towels, and bedding on a routine schedule.

## Home hygiene basics

- Clean high-touch surfaces (doorknobs, phones, keyboards, counters) frequently.
- Separate raw and cooked foods in the kitchen.
- Refrigerate perishable food promptly.
- Keep bathrooms dry/ventilated to reduce mold.
- Dispose of trash regularly.

## Community hygiene mindset

- Stay home when sick if possible.
- Keep shared spaces tidy after use.
- Practice respectful hygiene in public settings (gyms, schools, offices).

Consistency matters more than perfection: small daily routines prevent bigger problems.
