# Cocoa Drink: Comfort in a Cup

Rich cocoa can be a cozy pick-me-up or a nourishing snack. The key is balancing flavor, sweetness, and texture while keeping the process simple.

## Core Formula
- **Base**: 250 ml of milk or barista-style oat milk.
- **Cocoa**: 2 teaspoons of natural cocoa powder; sift to prevent lumps.
- **Sweetener**: 1–2 teaspoons of sugar, honey, or maple syrup.
- **Salt**: A tiny pinch to round out bitterness.
- **Heat**: Warm to 65–70°C (steaming but not boiling) to protect aroma.

## Optional Enhancements
- **Spices**: A dash of cinnamon for warmth, or cardamom for a floral edge.
- **Citrus**: Orange zest brightens the cup; add after heating to keep oils fresh.
- **Texture**: Whisk with a handheld frother for microfoam, or blend with ice for a chilled version.
- **Protein**: Stir in a spoonful of peanut butter or protein powder for a filling snack.
- **Bittersweet twist**: Add one square of dark chocolate to melt at the end.

## Quick Method
1. Heat the milk with salt and sweetener until small bubbles form at the edge.
2. In a mug, whisk cocoa powder with a splash of warm milk to form a paste—this prevents graininess.
3. Pour in the rest of the milk while whisking. Adjust sweetness, then finish with any enhancements.

## Serving Ideas
- Pair with toasted sourdough or banana slices for a balanced snack.
- Sprinkle flaky salt or cocoa nibs on top for crunch.
- For late-night wind-downs, swap half the milk for hot water to lighten the drink.
