# Invisalign overview

Invisalign is a brand of clear aligners used to straighten teeth without traditional braces. The system uses a sequence of custom-made, transparent trays that gradually move teeth into planned positions. Trays are removable, allowing patients to eat, drink, and maintain oral hygiene more easily than with fixed brackets.

## How the treatment works

1. **Digital planning** – Dentists or orthodontists take a 3D scan or impressions of the patient’s teeth. Specialized software maps the desired tooth movements and generates a series of aligners.
2. **Aligner series** – Each set of aligners is worn for about one to two weeks, applying gentle force to shift teeth. Patients typically wear them 20–22 hours per day for effective results.
3. **Monitoring** – Regular check-ins ensure movements track to the plan. If teeth are off-track, mid-course corrections or refinement aligners may be added.

## Advantages and considerations

- **Aesthetics** – Clear trays are less noticeable than metal braces, appealing for patients seeking a discreet option.
- **Comfort and hygiene** – Smooth plastic trays usually cause less irritation, and removal during meals supports better brushing and flossing.
- **Discipline required** – Success depends on consistent wear time; forgetting to wear aligners can prolong treatment.
- **Limitations** – Complex tooth rotations or large vertical movements might still require attachments, elastics, or other orthodontic techniques.

## Typical patient guidance

- Wear aligners for the prescribed duration daily and switch to the next set on schedule.
- Remove aligners only for eating, drinking anything besides water, and oral care.
- Clean aligners with lukewarm water and a soft brush; avoid hot water to prevent warping.
- Store aligners in their case when not worn to reduce loss or damage.

## Outcomes

Treatment lengths vary with complexity, often ranging from several months to over a year. When worn as directed, Invisalign can address crowding, spacing, and mild bite issues while offering an unobtrusive appearance and flexible lifestyle.
