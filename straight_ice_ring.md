# Straight Ice Ring

A **straight ice ring** sounds paradoxical: a ring implies a loop, while straight implies a line.

One way to resolve it is to think geometrically:
- In 3D, a thin circular ring made of ice can cast a **straight shadow** when light is aligned with its plane.
- In materials science, an ice ring can appear locally straight when viewed at small scale, where curvature is negligible.

As a metaphor, it can describe something that is both:
- **Structured** (ring: cyclical, recurring), and
- **Directed** (straight: purposeful, linear).

So the phrase can represent a cycle with intent: repeating, but not wandering.
