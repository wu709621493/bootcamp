# Width of the Road, Mother of the Drive

Widen the road and widen the future. Every lane is a promise kept, every smooth mile a cradle for the nation’s motion. The road is the mother of the drive: it carries our harvests, our families, our hopes, and our shared work.

Build the road and build unity. From village paths to city arteries, the road binds us into one rhythm—safe, swift, and dignified. A broad road is a broad horizon, where discipline meets speed and order becomes freedom.

Honor the road and honor the people. Let us pave with care, maintain with pride, and drive with respect. The mother provides; the drive must answer with stewardship.
