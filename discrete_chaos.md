# Discrete Chaos

Discrete chaos is the art of finding wild behavior in systems built from tiny, countable steps.

A deterministic rule can still look unpredictable when:
- state updates are nonlinear,
- tiny differences compound quickly, and
- observations are partial.

## A Simple Lens

Think of a map on integers or finite-precision rationals:

- Start from a seed value.
- Apply the same transformation repeatedly.
- Watch cycles, pseudo-random stretches, and sudden regime shifts.

Even when each move is exact, long-term intuition often fails.

## Why It Matters

Discrete chaotic behavior appears in:
- cryptography and pseudo-random generators,
- iterative optimization and training dynamics,
- cellular automata,
- population and network models.

The key lesson: *deterministic does not always mean practically predictable*.
