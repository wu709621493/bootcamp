# MotoGP

MotoGP is the premier class of Grand Prix motorcycle racing, featuring the fastest prototypes built by manufacturers such as Ducati, Honda, Yamaha, Aprilia, and KTM. It is the top tier of the FIM Road Racing World Championship Grand Prix (commonly called the Grand Prix World Championship).

## A brief history

Modern MotoGP traces its roots to the inaugural 1949 championship. Over the decades, the sport evolved from 500cc two-strokes to today’s four-stroke 1000cc prototypes, with significant advances in engine performance, aerodynamics, and rider safety. The “MotoGP” name officially became the premier-class branding in 2002.

## The racing classes

A Grand Prix weekend typically features three classes:

- **MotoGP**: The premier class with 1000cc prototype machines.
- **Moto2**: A middle class focused on chassis development with standardized engines.
- **Moto3**: The lightweight class, often the first step for rising talent.

## Weekend format

A typical event spans three days:

1. **Friday**: Practice sessions to build pace and gather data.
2. **Saturday**: Additional practice plus qualifying to set the grid.
3. **Sunday**: Main race for each class; some events also include a Saturday sprint race for MotoGP.

## What makes MotoGP unique

- **Prototype engineering**: Bikes are not road-legal models; they are purpose-built racing machines.
- **Close racing**: Tire management, braking prowess, and racecraft often create thrilling last-lap battles.
- **Rider skill**: MotoGP demands extreme precision, courage, and physical conditioning.

## Technology highlights

- **Aerodynamics**: Winglets and fairings optimize stability and cornering grip.
- **Electronics**: Advanced traction control and ride-by-wire manage power delivery.
- **Brakes and tires**: Carbon brakes and specialized compounds enable late braking and high cornering speeds.

## How to follow the sport

Fans can follow the championship via official broadcasts, race weekend highlights, and team updates throughout the season. Schedules, standings, and rider news evolve rapidly across a long, global calendar.

## Why it captivates fans

MotoGP blends engineering innovation with elite athletic performance. Each race is a balance between risk and finesse, making every lap unpredictable and every championship a story of resilience, strategy, and speed.
