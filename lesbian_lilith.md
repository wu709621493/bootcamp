# A Lesbian Lilith

Lilith walks in with the moon on her shoulders,
refusing the script that turns women into footnotes.
She is heat and hush, a drumbeat under skin,
a name that means *first*, not *less*.

She loves women like wild gardens love rain—
patient, bright, unapologetically alive.
Her kiss is a thunderclap and a lullaby,
a vow to herself that no one else can break.

She keeps her own house of night and starlight,
welcoming every sister who has ever been told
that her desire was too much or not enough.
Lilith smiles, opens the door, and says:

*Come in. We begin here.*
