# Envy as Directional Signal

Envy points toward qualities, relationships, or achievements we value but have not yet claimed. Instead of treating it as moral failure, noticing the direction of envy can guide choices that align with our own values.

## Key Points
- **Locator of desire:** Envy often reveals neglected aspirations or stalled ambitions.
- **Contrast without collapse:** Acknowledge another's success without denying your own progress or context.
- **Action over comparison:** Translate the discomfort into specific, reachable steps rather than vague self-critique.
- **Guardrails:** Avoid imitation that erodes integrity; focus on practices that cultivate your version of the admired trait.

Envy loses its grip when transformed into a map: it shows where you want to go, but you still choose the path and pace that suit your life.
