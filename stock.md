# Stock Market Snapshot Cheat Sheet

A quick reference for tracking and interpreting stock positions, portfolio health, and market context.

## Core metrics
- **Price action**: current price, day change (%), 52-week range.
- **Volume pulse**: compare today’s volume to 20-day average to spot unusual activity.
- **Volatility**: 30-day historical volatility and implied volatility if options are liquid.
- **Liquidity check**: average bid-ask spread and typical dollar volume to gauge trading friction.

## Fundamentals to glance at
- **Earnings power**: trailing and forward P/E, EPS growth over 1–3 years.
- **Quality**: return on invested capital, gross margin trend, and free cash flow conversion.
- **Balance sheet**: net debt/EBITDA and interest coverage for solvency risk.
- **Capital returns**: dividend yield, payout ratio, and buyback cadence.

## Position sizing
- Define max risk per position (e.g., 0.5%–1% of portfolio value).
- Convert risk to shares: `shares = (risk_amount) / (entry_price - stop_price)`.
- Favor tighter stops and smaller size in high-volatility names.

## Entry and exit triggers
- **Momentum entry**: price above 50- and 200-day moving averages with rising volume.
- **Mean-revert entry**: bounce from support with RSI < 40 improving toward 50.
- **Stop discipline**: honor initial stop-loss; trail stops below higher lows after breakouts.
- **Profit taking**: scale out near prior resistance or when risk/reward falls below 1:1.

## Earnings and event playbook
- Avoid new positions within 48–72 hours of earnings unless position is explicitly event-driven.
- For earnings holds, reduce size so worst-case gap risk fits the max-risk rule.
- Track corporate actions (splits, special dividends, secondary offerings) before sizing.

## Portfolio hygiene
- Cap single-name exposure (e.g., <10%) and sector exposure (e.g., <25%).
- Rebalance monthly or after ±5% drift from target weights.
- Keep a cash buffer if volatility regime is rising (e.g., VIX > 25).

## Daily checklist
- Update watchlist notes and adjust alerts.
- Log entries/exits with rationale and chart context.
- Review overnight news for holdings and top watchlist names.
- Confirm position sizing and stops still align with account equity.
