# Health

Health is the ongoing capacity of body and mind to adapt to stressors and maintain balance. The foundation is simple:

- **Sleep** restores cognition, hormone balance, and immune readiness. Seven to nine hours on a consistent schedule is the anchor habit.
- **Nutrition** emphasizes vegetables, fruits, legumes, and whole grains, paired with adequate protein and unsaturated fats. Hydrate to thirst and limit ultra-processed foods.
- **Movement** blends aerobic activity, strength training, and mobility work to support cardiovascular function, insulin sensitivity, and joint health.
- **Stress regulation** uses practices like breath pacing, brief walks, and social connection to keep sympathetic arousal in check.
- **Preventive care** includes vaccines, regular checkups, and appropriate screening tests based on age, sex, and risk factors.
- **Environment** matters: good air quality, sunlight during the day, low noise at night, and ergonomic workspaces reduce chronic strain.

## Daily checklist

1. Go outside within an hour of waking to anchor circadian rhythms.
2. Drink water with meals and carry a bottle during active hours.
3. Accumulate at least 150 minutes per week of moderate aerobic activity plus two strength sessions.
4. Prioritize fiber-rich foods at each meal and include protein in snacks to stabilize energy.
5. Take short movement or breathing breaks every 60–90 minutes of desk work.
6. Power down bright screens 30–60 minutes before bed and keep the room cool and dark.
7. Plan and schedule preventive visits (dental, vision, primary care) annually.

## When to seek medical advice

- New chest pain, severe shortness of breath, neurologic changes (weakness, speech difficulty), or sudden severe headache.
- Persistent fever, unintentional weight loss, bleeding that does not stop, or wounds that will not heal.
- Mental health concerns such as hopelessness, intrusive thoughts, or social withdrawal.

These notes offer general guidance and are not a substitute for personalized medical advice from licensed clinicians.
