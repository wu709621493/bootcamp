# Trauma as an Unfinished Conversation

Trauma interrupts the flow of a person's story, leaving memories and bodily reactions that replay without context. Healing works like reintroducing dialogue between past and present selves.

## What Trauma Does
- **Freezes time:** Sensory fragments repeat because they were never safely integrated.
- **Shrinks choice:** Hypervigilance and avoidance narrow the range of options, reinforcing isolation.
- **Distorts identity:** Shame and helplessness seep into self-concept, making survival strategies feel like personality traits.

## What Helps
- **Predictable safety:** Consistent routines and informed caregivers teach the nervous system that calm is reliable, not rare.
- **Shared language:** Naming sensations and triggers turns implicit fear into something that can be planned around.
- **Body alongside mind:** Movement, breathwork, and regulated touch let physiology learn the danger has passed, complementing talk-based care.
- **Systems repair:** Stable housing, legal protections, and trustworthy institutions reduce ongoing threats so recovery is not constantly reset.

Trauma is not just remembered; it is re-lived until safety, meaning, and community allow the story to move forward again.
