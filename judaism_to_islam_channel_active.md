# Judaism to Islam Channel: Active

This channel is now active for respectful, structured interfaith exchange between Judaism and Islam.

## Purpose
- Build understanding through shared values, history, and lived practice.
- Reduce misconceptions by prioritizing primary sources and fair summaries.
- Support collaborative dialogue on ethics, community, and peacebuilding.

## Shared Foundations to Anchor Dialogue
- **Monotheism:** devotion to one God.
- **Revelation and scripture:** Torah and Qur'an as central sacred texts.
- **Law and practice:** halakhic and fiqh traditions shaping daily life.
- **Prayer, charity, and fasting:** recurring disciplines of worship and responsibility.
- **Mercy and justice:** core moral themes in both traditions.

## Operating Principles
1. Use charitable interpretation before critique.
2. Distinguish theology, law, culture, and politics.
3. Avoid collective blame; discuss ideas, not stereotypes.
4. Cite sources when making historical or doctrinal claims.
5. Prioritize safety, dignity, and de-escalation in disagreements.

## Suggested Discussion Tracks
- Prophets in shared and distinct narratives.
- Covenant, community, and religious law.
- Dietary practice, purity, and ritual life.
- Medieval Jewish-Muslim intellectual exchange.
- Contemporary coexistence and conflict transformation.

## First Session Prompt
"Identify one value both traditions hold deeply, then compare how it is expressed in text, ritual, and community life."
