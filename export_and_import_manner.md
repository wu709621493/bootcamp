# Export and Import Manner

## Purpose
Export and import manners describe the shared etiquette, documentation, and process norms that make cross-border trade reliable and respectful. Clear manners reduce friction, prevent costly delays, and create predictability for buyers, sellers, and regulators.

## Export manner
1. **Prepare compliant documentation**
   - Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and any product-specific permits.
   - Ensure descriptions match product codes and declared values.
2. **Respect destination standards**
   - Verify safety, labeling, and language requirements.
   - Use internationally recognized markings when applicable.
3. **Communicate shipment intent early**
   - Share timelines, carrier details, and tracking information.
   - Notify of any changes to quantities, packaging, or delivery terms.
4. **Package with care and transparency**
   - Use packaging that protects goods and allows inspection.
   - Label cartons clearly with consignee, origin, and handling instructions.
5. **Honor agreed trade terms**
   - Align with Incoterms on risk transfer, insurance, and costs.
   - Provide proof of export where required.

## Import manner
1. **Know the regulatory landscape**
   - Understand tariff schedules, quotas, and restricted items.
   - Arrange licenses and pre-approvals before shipment arrival.
2. **Plan for customs efficiency**
   - Use correct HS codes and avoid ambiguous descriptions.
   - Prepare payment of duties, taxes, and inspection fees.
3. **Maintain transparent communication**
   - Confirm receiving capacity and required delivery windows.
   - Provide clear contacts for inspections and documentation follow-up.
4. **Inspect responsibly**
   - Conduct checks promptly to minimize demurrage.
   - Document discrepancies with photos and detailed notes.
5. **Resolve issues respectfully**
   - Follow agreed dispute procedures for shortages or damage.
   - Share corrective actions and timelines.

## Shared manners for both sides
- **Accuracy over speed**: Accurate invoices and data reduce delays.
- **Cultural respect**: Use polite, clear language and honor local holidays.
- **Data security**: Protect sensitive trade documents and pricing data.
- **Sustainability**: Favor recyclable packaging and avoid wasteful practices.
- **Continuous improvement**: Post-transaction reviews build trust.

## Checklist (quick reference)
- Documentation complete and consistent
- Trade terms clearly stated
- Packaging and labeling aligned with destination requirements
- Duties and taxes planned
- Communication channels confirmed

By practicing consistent export and import manners, partners build durable relationships, lower risk, and keep goods moving smoothly across borders.
