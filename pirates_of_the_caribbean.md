# Pirates of the Caribbean

## Overview
"Pirates of the Caribbean" is a fantasy swashbuckling film series that blends supernatural mythology with maritime adventure. The films follow shifting alliances among pirates, navies, and mythic forces while centering on themes of freedom, loyalty, and the cost of ambition.

## Core motifs
- **The sea as destiny:** The ocean functions as both a setting and a character, shaping choices and fate.
- **Cursed bargains:** Deals for immortality or power often come with moral or spiritual consequences.
- **Shifting allegiances:** Characters repeatedly renegotiate loyalty, highlighting the tension between self-interest and honor.
- **Mythic creatures and lore:** The series draws on legends such as ghost ships, sea monsters, and ancient artifacts.

## Notable characters
- **Captain Jack Sparrow:** A trickster-hero whose improvisation and charm mask deeper cunning.
- **Will Turner:** A skilled swordsman driven by love and a desire for justice.
- **Elizabeth Swann:** A strategist who grows from aristocracy into leadership.
- **Hector Barbossa:** A pirate captain whose pragmatism makes him both ally and adversary.

## Why it resonates
The franchise combines spectacle with recognizable human stakes: the longing for freedom, the fear of betrayal, and the quest for identity. Its blend of humor and myth keeps the tone adventurous while leaving room for darker undertones.
