# "Kamchatka" Cranberry Flavored Kombucha

A tart cranberry kombucha concept inspired by Kamchatka:

- Flavor profile: bright cranberry acidity, gentle tea tannins, and a clean fermented finish.
- Aroma: wild berry, subtle floral notes, and a crisp, cold-air freshness.
- Serving suggestion: chill well and pair with smoked fish, rye bread, or sharp cheese.
