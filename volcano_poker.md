# Volcano Poker

Volcano poker is the art of reading pressure before it blows. The table is a caldera, the chips are minutes, and every player holds a silent stake in whether the hand erupts.

## The table
- **Dealer**: the mountain itself, shuffling magma, gas, and groundwater like cards none of us can see directly.
- **Players**: residents, scientists, emergency crews, farmers, airlines, and distant investors whose logistics run through ash-prone skies.
- **Pot**: the shared future—homes standing, crops alive, clean air, and trust in public institutions.

## Opening tell: baseline discipline
1. **Know the deck**: Build dense sensor networks—seismic arrays, infrasound, GNSS, gas sniffers—and keep them calibrated. Poker begins with knowing the cards in play.
2. **Memory as strategy**: Log every micro-signal and correlate it to past hands (historic unrest, phreatic pops, flank slips). Patterns become odds, odds become policy.
3. **Table minimums**: Fund the boring stuff: battery swaps, data backups, spare antennas, bilingual signage. Nothing tanks a hand faster than a dead modem.

## Betting rounds: escalating signals
- **Pre-flop (background rumble)**: Publish weekly public dashboards. No drama—just receipts that the game is watched.
- **Flop (gas spikes, small quakes)**: Move from dashboards to drills. Push SMS/WhatsApp/radio scripts that say what *will* happen next, not just what *is* happening.
- **Turn (deformation + harmonic tremor)**: Stage buses and livestock trailers at pre-agreed depots. Convert schools to clean-air shelters; wet down ash-prone roofs.
- **River (eruption probable)**: Execute lane reversals and lahar detours. Stream signage for masks and eye protection. Pre-clear insurance documents and microgrant forms so people can bet on staying safe, not on bureaucracy.

## Bluff detection
- **False alarms**: Accept them as antes paid for trust. Always show the cards—postmortems in plain language keep the table honest.
- **Rumor calls**: Track misinfo keywords, pre-bunk with local voices, and keep mayors, midwives, and market vendors as message multipliers.

## Fold conditions
- If evacuation times exceed modeled ashfall arrival by more than 20 minutes, fold into stay-shelter plans rather than clogged roads.
- If medical oxygen or dialysis buffers drop below 48 hours, trigger priority airlifts before airfields ash over.

## Winning the hand
You win volcano poker not by outsmarting the mountain, but by out-organizing chaos. Every quiet day refills the stack: repaired sirens, clearer maps, tighter community ties. The jackpot is boring—uneventful evacuations, roofs that stay up, and a public that believes the next alert is worth acting on.
