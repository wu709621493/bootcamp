# Capibara

The capibara is a calm giant of the wetlands, the world’s largest rodent and a patient neighbor to almost everything that shares its water. It moves with deliberate ease, preferring steady paths over sudden leaps, and its quiet presence makes space for other lives to gather.

## What it teaches
- **Composure.** Capibaras rest in warm shallows to regulate heat, showing how stillness can be a form of care.
- **Community.** They live in social groups that rely on mutual awareness, reminding us that belonging is a daily practice.
- **Adaptability.** Equally at home on land and in water, the capibara demonstrates how flexibility can be a kind of strength.

In a world that often rushes, the capibara offers a blueprint for steady pace, shared safety, and unhurried confidence.
