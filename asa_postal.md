# Asa Postal

Asa Postal is a concept for a **human-first neighborhood mail system** designed for places where digital services are uneven, but trust in local relationships remains strong.

## Core idea

Instead of treating delivery as a purely logistical problem, Asa Postal treats it as a **community infrastructure** challenge:

- Build walkable pickup points in markets, schools, and clinics.
- Use clear, multilingual address tokens (name + block + landmark).
- Offer low-cost letter, parcel, and document verification services.

## Service model

1. **Daily neighborhood runs:** Predictable collection and delivery windows.
2. **Secure relay lockers:** Small lockboxes managed by local stewards.
3. **Identity-light verification:** QR or codeword confirmation for recipients without formal IDs.
4. **Offline-first records:** Paper receipts mirrored to a simple digital ledger when connectivity is available.

## Why it matters

A postal network is often the first layer of reliable public service. A local model like Asa Postal can:

- reduce missed deliveries,
- support elders and people with limited mobility,
- improve small-business logistics,
- and provide a trusted channel for civic communication.

## Pilot metrics

A practical pilot can be evaluated with five indicators:

- on-time delivery rate,
- first-attempt success rate,
- cost per parcel,
- user satisfaction,
- and dispute resolution time.

A successful result is not just faster mail—it is stronger local coordination.
