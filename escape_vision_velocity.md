# Escape Vision Velocity

Escape vision velocity is the speed at which perception outruns fear.

It begins when the eye stops asking, *"What if I fall?"* and starts asking, *"What if I can steer?"* At low speeds, we confuse detail for truth. Every crack in the road feels fatal, every shadow a warning. But as momentum builds, noise thins. Patterns emerge. Horizons organize chaos.

To reach escape vision velocity:

1. **Name the gravity.** Know what holds you down—habit, doubt, old stories.
2. **Align thrust with intent.** Effort without direction burns fuel; direction without effort is a map with no traveler.
3. **Accept blur at the edges.** Not every variable can be tracked. Clarity lives at the centerline.
4. **Commit past hesitation.** The launch window closes for those waiting to feel ready.

At escape vision velocity, you do not stop seeing risk—you stop worshiping it.
You trade panic for trajectory.
You stop orbiting the life you meant to start.
