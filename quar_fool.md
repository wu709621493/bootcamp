# Quar Fool

Quar Fool is the small, stubborn spark that argues with the quartered clock.
It shows up at the edge of a season and says the calendar is a polite rumor,
not a ruler. It is the voice that laughs at deadlines without mocking the work,
insisting that time is a partner, not a warden.

## Traits

- **Rhythmic defiance:** Keeps a private beat while still honoring the shared song.
- **Gentle mischief:** Refuses to let seriousness calcify into fear.
- **Seasonal honesty:** Names when the quarter no longer fits the lived weather.

## Practice

1. Pause at the quarter mark and ask, “What changed that the plan did not?”
2. Adjust the map, not the spirit of the journey.
3. Celebrate the small reroutes that kept the mission alive.

Quar Fool is a reminder that adaptation is not failure; it is fidelity to reality.
