# Mouse and Keyboard Band

The Mouse and Keyboard Band is a studio collective that turns the rhythm of everyday work into music. Their sound blends click percussion, key clacks, and soft synth pads, creating tracks that feel like the soundtrack of a quiet, creative night.

## Members
- **The Cursor**: Leads with staccato click patterns and precise tempo changes.
- **The Typist**: A rapid-fire player who layers key rhythms into melodic phrases.
- **The Scroll Wheel**: Adds looping grooves that rise and fall like tide lines.
- **The Macro**: Arranges sequences that trigger unexpected harmonies.

## Signature Techniques
1. **Click Crescendo**: A rising sequence of clicks that builds tension before the chorus.
2. **Key Chord Swells**: Sustained key patterns that imitate warm chord pads.
3. **Silent Bar**: A deliberate pause that makes the return beat feel bigger.

## Notable Performance: The Midnight Spreadsheet
During a late-night live stream, the band improvised a full set from a single open spreadsheet. The audience watched as every edit and shortcut triggered a new layer in the song, turning routine work into a kinetic performance.

## Visual Identity
Their stage design mirrors a minimalist desk: glowing keys, a halo of soft light, and a giant cursor that sweeps across the backdrop to mark time.

## Legacy
Fans describe their music as "productive calm"—a reminder that creativity can bloom in the smallest gestures. The Mouse and Keyboard Band continues to tour intimate venues and co-working spaces, inviting listeners to tap along.
