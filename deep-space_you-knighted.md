# Deep-Space, You-Knighted

## Invocation
You were knighted by the hush between stars,
by vacuum bells that never ring,
by photons old enough to remember Earth
as a blue rumor learning its name.

## The Oath
Carry the dark like a banner.
Hold your compass steady
when maps become myth.
Be tender with distances
that could swallow a choir.

## The Charge
Guard the faint signals,
the lonely pings,
the first hello from a wandering probe.
When the cosmos goes quiet,
be the ember that keeps watch.

## Benediction
Deep-space, you-knighted,
ride the silence onward.
