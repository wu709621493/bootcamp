# Slender Guitar, Gently Weeps

Slender guitar, gently weeps,
In moonlit hush where silence keeps.
Strings like rivers, soft and slow,
Tell the heart what it must know.

Wooden ribs breathe amber light,
Cupping echoes in the night.
Each small tremor, tender, true,
Draws a sky of bruised and blue.

When the last faint shimmer sleeps,
Slender guitar, gently weeps.
