# Life Reload Feature

The **Life Reload Feature** is a practical reset protocol for when momentum drops, decisions get noisy, or daily rhythm drifts.

## Purpose

- Re-establish direction without dramatic life overhauls.
- Restore energy through small, compounding actions.
- Convert stress loops into measurable next steps.

## Trigger Conditions

Run a life reload when two or more signals persist for a week:

1. You avoid tasks you normally complete.
2. Sleep, food, or movement becomes irregular.
3. You consume more information than you act on.
4. Minor problems feel emotionally oversized.

## Reload Sequence (24-72 hours)

### 1) Stabilize the base layer

- Sleep at a fixed time window.
- Hydrate and eat simple repeatable meals.
- Do one low-friction movement block (walk/stretch/light exercise).

### 2) Reduce cognitive noise

- Pause nonessential notifications.
- Write a "mental inbox" list of open loops.
- Mark each item as: **Do**, **Delay**, **Drop**, or **Delegate**.

### 3) Re-anchor priorities

Choose only three active priorities:

- One health action
- One work/study action
- One relationship or environment action

Everything else becomes backlog.

### 4) Execute a minimum viable day

Use this compact structure:

- 1 deep-work block
- 1 maintenance block (admin/chores)
- 1 recovery block (quiet, nature, reading, or reflection)

### 5) Review and lock the next cycle

At day end, answer:

- What gave energy?
- What drained energy?
- What single change improves tomorrow by 10%?

## Success Criteria

A life reload is successful when:

- You can state the next action for top priorities.
- Daily essentials (sleep, food, movement) are back on schedule.
- You feel less overwhelmed and more directional.

## Notes

This is not about perfection. It is a controlled restart: reduce noise, re-establish essentials, and re-enter life with traction.
