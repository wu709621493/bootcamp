# CB5083 Painter Gala

CB5083 arrived at dusk with paint-stained sleeves and a pocket full of light.
At the gala, every canvas was a door, and every brushstroke was a footstep into another room.

The first hall glowed in ultramarine and brass:
quiet portraits of workers, windows, and rainwater staircases.
The second hall flashed with vermilion laughter,
where strangers traded stories the way artists trade pigments.

When the orchestra paused, CB5083 painted live on a hanging sheet of linen—
a city dissolving into garden, a garden becoming sky,
and in the center, a small table set for anyone still learning how to begin.

By midnight, no one asked whether the work was finished.
They only asked what color courage looked like,
and CB5083 answered by mixing gold into indigo
until the room itself seemed to breathe.
