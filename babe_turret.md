# Babe Turret

A babe turret is the paradox of softness and vigilance: a cradle that keeps watch without hardening the heart.
It stands for the kind of protection that is gentle, patient, and awake—a shelter that does not frighten the one it guards.

## Core ideas
- **Gentle strength:** Safety can be quiet, sustained by care rather than force.
- **Presence over power:** The turret is not a weapon, but a promise to remain nearby.
- **Growth-aware:** Protection adapts as the babe learns to see the world.

## A short reflection
Imagine a tower built from blankets and lullabies. Its stones are routines, its windows are trust,
and its signal fires are small daily acts: a warmed bottle, a steady voice, a light left on.
That is the babe turret—defense by tenderness, watchfulness by love.
