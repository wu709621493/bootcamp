# Lime Bell

A bell cast from lime and salt air rings softly in the orchard twilight.
Its chime is citrus-bright: a kindness that wakes the quiet stones.
Every strike drips green light over the soil and seeds, reminding roots
that sound can be nourishment when the world tastes bitter.

The bell refuses rust by laughing at rain, minting echoes that roll
through rows of trees like a fresh-cut melody. Travelers pause, sip
its resonance like sherbet, and walk lighter, leaves in their pockets,
remembering that courage can be zested into any evening.
