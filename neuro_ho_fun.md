# Neuro Ho Fun

A wok-seared, saucy ho fun dish tuned for brain-friendly ingredients. It blends wide rice noodles with omega-3-rich salmon, mushrooms, and leafy greens for a satisfying bowl that leans into memory-boosting nutrients without sacrificing classic, smoky stir-fry comfort.

## Ingredients (Serves 2)
- 8 oz (225 g) fresh or dried ho fun (wide rice noodles)
- 6 oz (170 g) skinless salmon fillet, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 1 cup shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 scallions, sliced (white and green separated)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 2 tbsp avocado or neutral oil, divided
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds (optional)

## Sauce
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine or dry sherry
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp water

## Preparation
1. **Prep noodles:** If using dried ho fun, soak in hot water until pliable, then drain. If fresh, separate the sheets gently.
2. **Mix sauce:** Whisk together all sauce ingredients until the cornstarch dissolves.
3. **Season salmon:** Lightly season salmon with a pinch of salt.

## Cooking
1. **Sear salmon:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high. Sear salmon 1–2 minutes per side until lightly browned, then transfer to a plate.
2. **Aromatics:** Add remaining oil, garlic, ginger, and scallion whites. Stir-fry 15–20 seconds until fragrant.
3. **Vegetables:** Add mushrooms and bell pepper. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until the mushrooms release moisture and brown.
4. **Noodles + sauce:** Add ho fun and drizzle the sauce around the wok edge. Toss gently to coat and let the sauce thicken, about 1 minute.
5. **Finish:** Return salmon, add spinach, and toss until the spinach wilts and salmon is warmed through. Remove from heat and drizzle with sesame oil.

## Serving
- Top with scallion greens and sesame seeds.
- Serve immediately for maximum wok hei.

## Brain-Friendly Notes
- **Omega-3s:** Salmon offers DHA and EPA, supportive of neuronal membranes.
- **Mushrooms:** Shiitakes bring B vitamins and umami depth.
- **Leafy greens:** Spinach adds folate and antioxidants.

## Tips
- **Avoid tearing noodles:** Use a wide spatula or tongs and toss gently.
- **High heat, fast finish:** Keep ingredients prepped so the stir-fry stays hot and quick.
