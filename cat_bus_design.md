Cat Bus Design

Overview
The cat bus is a whimsical, eco-friendly transit vehicle that merges feline comfort with public transportation practicality. It serves as a neighborhood shuttle, tourist attraction, and community art piece while remaining fully accessible and safe.

Design Goals
- Create a recognizable, friendly silhouette that feels like a giant, resting cat.
- Prioritize accessibility, safety, and quiet operation.
- Use sustainable materials and low-noise electric propulsion.

Exterior Concept
- Body: Elongated, rounded cabin with soft curves mimicking a cat’s torso.
- Windows: Oval “whisker” windows along the sides with subtle etched whisker lines.
- Front: A smiling cat face with LED “eyes” used as turn indicators.
- Roof: Solar panel “fur” tiles with a matte texture that reduces glare.
- Wheels: Hidden by stylized “paws” that move slightly as suspension articulates.

Interior Layout
- Capacity: 18 seated, 10 standing.
- Seating: Side benches upholstered in durable, warm-toned fabric resembling fur.
- Aisle: Wide center path for mobility devices and strollers.
- Grab Points: Curved, “tail” shaped handrails overhead.
- Lighting: Soft, amber LED strips with adjustable brightness.

Accessibility Features
- Low-floor entry with automatic ramp.
- Dedicated wheelchair space with securement points.
- Visual and audio stop announcements.
- High-contrast floor markings for navigation.

Safety and Operations
- 360-degree cameras and proximity sensors.
- Speed limited to neighborhood-safe ranges (max 25 mph).
- Emergency exits marked with paw-print icons.
- Fire-retardant interior materials and battery thermal management.

Sustainability
- Battery-electric drivetrain with regenerative braking.
- Recycled aluminum frame and bio-based interior panels.
- Solar roof supplements auxiliary systems (HVAC, lighting).

Color Palette
- Primary: Warm cream body.
- Accent: Deep charcoal for windows and trim.
- Highlights: Soft coral for nose, paw pads, and interior accents.

Community Integration
- Seasonal art wraps designed by local schools.
- QR codes for route info and local history.
- Quiet mode for early morning and late-night operations.
