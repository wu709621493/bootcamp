# Perpetual Motion Maze

The maze did not have walls.
It had intentions.

Every corridor promised an exit,
then gently curved back
into a memory of itself.

A brass pendulum swung at the center,
not to keep time,
but to keep hope alive.

"Forward," said the floor tiles,
clicking in patient sequence.
"Forward," echoed the ceiling,
with constellations that rearranged
whenever I looked away.

I marked each turn with chalk.
The marks moved overnight,
like obedient stars.

In chamber seven,
I found my own footsteps
arriving from the opposite side.
They bowed.
I did not.

In chamber twelve,
a wind-up atlas spun,
its countries folded into gears.
A river flowed uphill
only when unobserved.

At last,
I reached a door labeled END,
its hinges humming like a thesis.
Behind it:
room one.

The pendulum smiled,
if metal can smile,
and kept swinging between
almost
and again.

I understood then:
this maze was a machine
powered by unfinished attempts.

And I,
faithful particle,
its favorite fuel.
