Human sofa

A human sofa is a metaphor for living comfort: a person who offers presence,
patience, and warmth as a place to rest when the day is too heavy to carry.
Unlike furniture, the human sofa listens, adjusts, and helps you find a soft
landing without losing their own shape.

Use it gently. Everyone who holds space also needs space. Resting on someone
means respecting their boundaries, sharing the weight, and remembering to stand
up again so they can breathe.
