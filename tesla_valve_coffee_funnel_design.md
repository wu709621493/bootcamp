# Tesla Valve Coffee Funnel Design

## Overview
A Tesla valve coffee funnel uses a passive, no-moving-parts, asymmetric flow path to encourage steady forward flow of brewed coffee while damping backflow or turbulence when the user pours too quickly or swirls. The result is a smoother extraction with reduced channeling and splashing, plus a built-in pre-infusion pathway that keeps grounds saturated more evenly.

## Functional Goals
- **Stable forward flow:** Encourage a consistent downward stream even during uneven pour rates.
- **Backflow suppression:** Reduce upward surges when the kettle stream pulses.
- **Gentle agitation:** Add mild lateral mixing without collapsing the coffee bed.
- **Easy cleaning:** All channels large enough to rinse without clogging.

## Geometry Concept
The funnel wall integrates a series of Tesla valve cells that create a preferred flow direction. Each cell is a looped side-channel that opens into the main path with a shallow angle in the forward direction and a sharper angle in reverse.

### Suggested Cell Layout
- **Cell count:** 8–12 cells around the circumference.
- **Cell pitch:** 8–10 mm vertical spacing.
- **Side-channel width:** 2.5–3.5 mm.
- **Main channel width:** 6–8 mm.
- **Inlet angle (forward):** 15–25°.
- **Reverse angle:** 45–60°.

## Flow Path Summary
1. Water enters the funnel and spreads across the bed.
2. Brewed coffee exits through the base and encounters the Tesla valve array.
3. In the forward direction, the looped channels smoothly rejoin the main path.
4. Any reverse pulses are forced into the loops, losing energy before reentering.

## Materials
- **Body:** Food-safe PPSU or stainless steel with a brushed finish.
- **Surface finish:** 0.8–1.2 µm Ra to reduce residue adhesion.
- **Seal ring:** Silicone O-ring at the filter-holder interface.

## Brewing Notes
- Best paired with flat-bottom filters to support even bed depth.
- Pour in slow circles to exploit the flow straightening effect.
- Slightly coarser grind may help the Tesla valve channels stay clear.

## Manufacturing Notes
- **Injection molding:** Preferred for PPSU with split tooling for the looped channels.
- **Metal version:** Use CNC milling with a two-part shell and laser welding.
- **Cleaning access:** Include a 4–5 mm vent slot at the base for rinse flow.

## Future Iterations
- Add a removable inner sleeve with different valve geometries.
- Integrate a flow-rate indicator window at the outlet.
- Explore spiral Tesla cells to increase mixing without extra height.
