# Bill

Bill is a steady, inquisitive engineer who balances reliability with curiosity. He keeps notes on what works, experiments with what might work next, and always leaves room for small daily joys.

## Quick facts
- **Profession:** Electrical engineer specializing in embedded systems and low-power design.
- **Location:** Portland, Oregon.
- **Signature habit:** Carries a pocket notebook for sketches and back-of-the-envelope calculations.
- **Motto:** "Test twice, trust once."

## Daily rhythm
1. **Morning warm-up:** A five-mile bike commute that doubles as a brainstorming session.
2. **Focus block:** Schematics, firmware tweaks, and quick bench measurements before lunch.
3. **Afternoon collaboration:** Code reviews and design reviews, translating lab findings into clear action items.
4. **Evening reset:** Cooking a new recipe or fixing something mechanical around the house.

## Working style
- Breaks complex problems into layered prototypes, starting with the minimum circuit or the smallest firmware loop.
- Writes concise documentation as he goes, preferring checklists and annotated diagrams over lengthy prose.
- Treats testing as part of design, not a separate phase, and keeps measurement logs reproducible.
- Appreciates teammates who surface edge cases early and ask clarifying questions.

## Current interests
- Low-noise analog front-ends for environmental sensors.
- Better observability in microcontroller firmware through lightweight trace points.
- Repairing vintage radios to understand how earlier engineers solved reliability problems with limited components.

## A note on collaboration
Bill is the person who volunteers to tidy the lab bench before a group build, not because tidiness is a rule, but because it lowers friction for everyone else. He believes small acts of consideration compound into reliable systems and reliable teams.
