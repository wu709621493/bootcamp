# Follow-me farm

## Overview
Follow-me farm is a concept for smallholder agriculture that uses gentle automation and shared logistics to let people, tools, and crops move together in a coordinated rhythm. The farm does not chase scale; it follows the farmer’s intent, weather, and soil signals. Think of it as a farm that “follows” the human plan rather than forcing humans to follow machines.

## Core principles
- **Human-first orchestration:** All automation is opt-in and reversible, with a clear manual override at every step.
- **Field-by-field mobility:** Lightweight modular stations (seed prep, irrigation, storage) can be moved as the season shifts.
- **Soil-respectful timing:** Operations are triggered by moisture, temperature, and compaction thresholds.
- **Community pooling:** Neighboring farms can share modules and transport routes, reducing idle equipment.

## Seasonal flow
1. **Spring:** Modules pivot to seedling protection and cold-night monitoring; a shared cart follows the row order each farmer sets.
2. **Summer:** Irrigation and shade netting are deployed in sequence, with sensors prompting the farmer to approve schedules.
3. **Autumn:** Harvest carts follow picked rows; storage modules are repositioned near collection points to minimize handling.
4. **Winter:** Tools and modules are serviced in a cooperative yard, with data reports prepared for next year’s planning.

## Benefits
- Reduced physical strain through collaborative logistics.
- Improved soil outcomes from timely, lighter-touch interventions.
- Lower capital risk by sharing mobile modules and transport.

## Closing note
Follow-me farm is a model for flexible, human-centered agriculture: the farm follows the people, not the other way around.
