# Guangzhou hospital entry / clinician office ID guidance

General practical guidance for Guangzhou (China):

1. **Bring a government-issued ID for any in-person outpatient visit.**
   - Mainland residents: PRC Resident Identity Card.
   - Foreign nationals: passport (and visa/residence document if requested).
   - Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan residents: travel permit / residence permit accepted by the hospital.

2. **For seeing a clinician in an outpatient office, ID is commonly required at registration/triage even if not always checked at the clinic-room door.**
   - Most hospitals run real-name registration and link records/payment to the patient identity.

3. **Building-entry screening can require ID in some hospitals/campuses, but this is hospital-specific and can change.**
   - Security may ask for patient appointment proof and companion details.

4. **If accompanying someone, carry your own ID too.**
   - Some facilities limit companion access and may require registration at security.

5. **Best practice before arrival:**
   - Check the specific hospital's official WeChat account or hotline for current "real-name registration" and visitor-entry rules.

Bottom line: **Assume a resident/government ID is required for clinician office access workflows in Guangzhou hospitals, at least at registration, and possibly again at building/area entry depending on the hospital.**
