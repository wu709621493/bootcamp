# Colleague Communication Code of Conduct

## Purpose
This code of conduct outlines how we communicate with colleagues so that collaboration remains respectful, clear, and productive.

## Core Principles
- **Respect every person.** Treat colleagues with dignity regardless of role, background, identity, or viewpoint.
- **Assume positive intent.** Start from the belief that others are trying to contribute in good faith.
- **Be clear and constructive.** Focus on ideas, outcomes, and specific behaviors rather than personal judgments.
- **Listen actively.** Make space for others, ask clarifying questions, and confirm understanding before responding.
- **Own your impact.** If your words cause harm, acknowledge it and work to repair trust.

## Communication Expectations
- Use professional, inclusive language in meetings, chat, email, documents, and code reviews.
- Disagree respectfully; challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Provide actionable feedback with examples and suggestions.
- Avoid sarcasm, insults, hostile humor, or dismissive language.
- Do not interrupt repeatedly or dominate discussions.
- Credit contributions and avoid claiming others' work.
- Keep sensitive topics and personal information confidential.

## Collaboration During Conflict
- Address issues early and directly with the people involved when safe to do so.
- Focus on facts, shared goals, and observable behavior.
- Pause heated conversations and resume with a calmer, solution-oriented approach.
- Invite a neutral facilitator (manager, team lead, or HR) if resolution stalls.

## Unacceptable Behavior
The following behaviors are not allowed:
- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, public shaming, or retaliatory behavior.
- Derogatory language related to identity or protected characteristics.
- Repeatedly ignoring boundaries, feedback, or established communication norms.

## Reporting and Accountability
- Report concerns to your manager, team lead, HR, or designated reporting channel.
- Reports will be reviewed promptly, respectfully, and as confidentially as possible.
- Violations may result in coaching, formal warnings, reassignment, or further disciplinary action.

## Commitment
By working together, we commit to creating a culture of trust, psychological safety, and mutual respect where everyone can do their best work.
