# Maid/Butler Concourse

A **maid/butler concourse** is a shared service corridor or support zone where hospitality staff stage, route, and coordinate tasks away from guest-facing spaces.

## Purpose

- Keep guest areas quiet, clean, and visually uncluttered.
- Speed up housekeeping and room-service turnover.
- Separate back-of-house logistics from front-of-house experience.

## Typical features

- Linen and amenity storage alcoves.
- Utility sinks and cleaning supply stations.
- Waste sorting points with odor control.
- Small dispatch desk or digital task board.
- Access control for staff-only circulation.

## Operational principles

1. **Discrete movement:** Use service paths that avoid guest bottlenecks.
2. **Time boxing:** Batch room refreshes by floor and time window.
3. **Readiness checks:** Restock carts and consumables at shift boundaries.
4. **Safety and hygiene:** Maintain clear walkways and labeled chemicals.

## Design notes

- Place concourses near elevator cores but acoustically buffered.
- Use durable, washable finishes with high slip resistance.
- Provide strong ventilation, bright task lighting, and clear signage.
- Reserve turning radius for carts to avoid traffic jams.

In short, the maid/butler concourse is the hidden coordination layer that sustains seamless guest service.
