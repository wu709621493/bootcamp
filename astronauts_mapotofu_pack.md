# Astronauts’ Mapotofu Pack

## Overview
This mapotofu (mapo tofu) pack is designed for make-ahead meals: a savory, spicy tofu-and-beef braise portioned into compact pouches that reheat quickly and ride well in lunch boxes, coolers, or “mission kits.” The sauce is thickened a bit more than usual so it stays clingy after chilling, and the tofu is gently simmered to keep the cubes intact.

## Ingredients (makes 4 packs)
- 1 1/2 lb (680 g) soft or medium tofu, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 6 oz (170 g) ground beef or pork
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
- 1 1/2 tbsp doubanjiang (spicy bean paste)
- 1 tbsp fermented black beans, rinsed and mashed (optional)
- 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) low-sodium chicken stock or water
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, toasted and ground (or 1/2 tsp ground)

### Pack add-ins (choose per pouch)
- Steamed jasmine rice or brown rice
- Blanched green beans, bok choy, or broccoli
- Chili crisp, for extra heat

## Method
1. **Drain and prep tofu.** Drain tofu on paper towels for 10 minutes to remove excess moisture.
2. **Brown the meat.** Heat oil in a wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground meat and cook until browned, breaking it up into small bits, 3–4 minutes.
3. **Build the base.** Add scallion whites, garlic, and ginger; stir 30 seconds. Stir in doubanjiang and black beans; cook 1 minute until fragrant and the oil turns red.
4. **Simmer the tofu.** Pour in stock, soy sauce, and sugar. Gently slide in tofu. Simmer 5–7 minutes, stirring carefully.
5. **Thicken.** Stir in cornstarch slurry and simmer 1–2 minutes until glossy. Off heat, add sesame oil and Sichuan peppercorns. Fold in scallion greens.

## Pack assembly
1. **Cool quickly.** Spread the mapotofu in a shallow pan and cool to room temperature within 30 minutes, then refrigerate until cold.
2. **Portion.** Divide into 4 containers or vacuum pouches (about 1 1/4 cups per pack).
3. **Add sides.** Layer rice and vegetables in separate compartments or separate small pouches for best texture.

## Reheat and serve
- **Microwave:** Vent the container and heat 2–3 minutes, stirring once.
- **Stovetop:** Warm in a small pot over medium heat with a splash of water.

## Storage
- **Refrigerator:** Up to 4 days.
- **Freezer:** Up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

## Variations
- **Vegetarian:** Swap meat for finely chopped mushrooms or crumbled tempeh.
- **Extra mellow:** Use mild doubanjiang and skip Sichuan peppercorns.
- **High-protein:** Add edamame or serve with quinoa.
