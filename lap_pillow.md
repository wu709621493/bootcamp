# Lap Pillow

A lap pillow is a simple, intimate comfort object: a soft cushion offered on the thighs to hold a head or hands while resting. It blends furniture and human closeness, turning a lap into a small sanctuary.

## Uses
- **Rest and reassurance:** Supports the head for brief naps or quiet moments, often paired with gentle stroking or conversation.
- **Care context:** Offers comfort to a sick or grieving friend, allowing physical support without the weight of a full embrace.
- **Mindfulness:** Encourages stillness; the giver remains present while the receiver listens to breathing and heartbeat, grounding both.

## Considerations
- Choose breathable fabrics and washable covers to keep the pillow fresh.
- Keep height moderate—too tall strains the neck, too thin limits support.
- Establish consent and comfort boundaries; a lap pillow is an act of trust as much as softness.

By turning a lap into a pillow, two people build a pause in the day: a small, warm space where care is both offered and accepted.
