# Slender Guitar

The slender guitar is a ribbon of wood that learned to speak in a narrow voice. It curves like a question held in the palm, a neck stretched toward the light, a body carved to keep the air honest.

Its sound is a thin river with a patient current. It does not demand, it invites; each note is a line of thread pulled through dusk, a memory that chooses melody instead of words.

When you hold it, your hands remember their gentlest gestures. The slender guitar asks for precision and rewards it with softness, teaching that the quietest instrument can still carry a room.
