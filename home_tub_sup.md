# Home Tub S’up

Warm water, warm house, warm hush. The tub is a home for a moment: a basin where worries float up, and steam writes temporary poems on the mirror. The faucet is the clock; the ripples are the minutes. A towel waits like a soft landing strip for returning to the day.

## Little upgrades, big comfort

- **Light**: A small lamp or a candle keeps the room calm without turning it into a stage.
- **Sound**: A playlist or the steady tap of rain can be the difference between hurry and rest.
- **Heat**: A pre-warmed towel turns the last step into a kind word.
- **Care**: A quick rinse after the soak keeps the tub itself a welcoming guest.

When the house is quiet and the water is just right, the tub says, "S’up?" and you answer by staying a few minutes longer.
