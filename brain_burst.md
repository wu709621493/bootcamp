# Brain Burst

Brain Burst is a rapid ideation exercise for capturing the spark of a new idea before it fades. The goal is to give thoughts a direct path from inspiration to documentation so they can be refined later without losing the original excitement.

## Quick-start checklist
- Choose a prompt or question that needs fast exploration.
- Set a tight timebox (5–10 minutes) to keep energy high.
- Jot down immediate associations, risks, and next steps without over-editing.
- Mark any open questions to revisit after the burst.
