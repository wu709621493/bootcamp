# Almond Shrimp

## Overview
Almond shrimp is a quick stir-fry that pairs sweet shrimp with a glossy savory sauce and a crunchy almond topping. It’s built around a brief marinade, a fast sear, and a final toss so the shrimp stay tender and the almonds stay crisp.

## Ingredients (serves 2–3)
- 1 lb (450 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp white pepper
- 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 1/2 cup snow peas, trimmed
- 1/2 cup red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1/3 cup sliced almonds

### Sauce
- 2 tbsp chicken stock (or water)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp oyster sauce
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp cornstarch

## Method
1. **Marinate shrimp:** Toss shrimp with Shaoxing wine, cornstarch, salt, and white pepper. Let stand for 10 minutes.
2. **Toast almonds:** Heat a wok or skillet over medium heat. Add almonds and toast until golden and fragrant, 2–3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Sear shrimp:** Increase heat to medium-high. Add 1 tbsp oil, then shrimp in a single layer. Sear 60–90 seconds per side until just pink. Remove to a plate.
4. **Stir-fry aromatics and vegetables:** Add remaining oil, then garlic and ginger. Stir for 15 seconds, then add snow peas and bell pepper. Stir-fry 1–2 minutes until crisp-tender.
5. **Finish sauce:** Stir sauce ingredients together. Add shrimp back to the wok, pour in sauce, and toss until thickened and glossy, 30–60 seconds.
6. **Garnish:** Fold in toasted almonds right before serving to keep them crisp.

## Serving suggestions
- Serve with steamed jasmine rice or a light noodle dish.
- Add a side of quick-sautéed greens (bok choy or gai lan).

## Variations
- **Almond-ginger shrimp:** Double the ginger and add a splash of rice vinegar.
- **Vegetable swap:** Replace snow peas with asparagus or broccoli florets.
- **Spicy:** Add 1–2 tsp chili crisp or a pinch of crushed red pepper.
