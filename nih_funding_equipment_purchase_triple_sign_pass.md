# NIH Funding Equipment Purchase Triple-Sign Pass

A **triple-sign pass** is a three-approval checkpoint for NIH-funded equipment purchases to improve compliance, budget control, and audit readiness.

## Purpose
- Confirm the equipment is allowable under the NIH award terms.
- Ensure the cost is necessary, reasonable, and allocable.
- Prevent single-person authorization risk for high-value purchases.

## Required Signatures
1. **Principal Investigator (PI)**
   - Confirms scientific necessity and alignment with the approved scope of work.
2. **Department/Unit Financial Authority**
   - Verifies available budget, correct cost allocation, and internal policy compliance.
3. **Sponsored Programs or Grants Compliance Office**
   - Confirms NIH terms, prior-approval requirements, and documentation completeness.

## Recommended Thresholds
- Apply triple-sign pass to:
  - Any single equipment purchase at or above the institution's capital threshold.
  - Any NIH-funded equipment purchase that may trigger sponsor prior approval.
  - Sensitive categories (shared core instruments, fabrication systems, or restricted-use devices).

## Minimum Documentation Packet
- Vendor quote(s) and procurement justification.
- Equipment justification linked to specific aims or project milestones.
- Budget line reference and current burn-rate snapshot.
- Sole-source rationale (if applicable).
- Asset tagging and stewardship plan.
- Installation, maintenance, and service contract details.

## Fast-Track Option
For time-critical replacements (for example, failed essential instruments), institutions may allow a **conditional pass**:
- PI + Financial Authority immediate sign-off.
- Compliance office review within a defined short window.
- Purchase order release remains contingent on final compliance confirmation.

## Audit-Ready Controls
- Store all approvals in a centralized system with timestamps.
- Require conflict-of-interest attestation for requestors and approvers.
- Log exceptions with corrective actions.
- Perform quarterly spot checks of NIH equipment purchases.

## One-Page Workflow
1. Request submitted with full documentation packet.
2. PI signs for scientific need.
3. Finance signs for budget and allowability mapping.
4. Compliance signs for NIH terms and prior-approval checks.
5. Procurement issues PO.
6. Receiving confirms delivery; asset management tags equipment.
7. Records retained per NIH and institutional retention schedules.

This model helps institutions move quickly while preserving sponsor compliance and financial accountability.
