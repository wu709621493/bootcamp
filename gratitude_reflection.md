# Gratitude Reflection

This note captures quick practices to cultivate gratitude throughout daily routines.

## Why it matters

* Lowers stress by redirecting attention toward steady, supportive parts of life.
* Builds patience when plans shift by highlighting what remained reliable.
* Strengthens relationships through explicit appreciation of others' efforts.

## Five-minute practice

1. **Breathe** for three deep cycles to settle your thoughts.
2. **List three specifics** you appreciated in the last 24 hours (people, moments, sensations).
3. **Name the support** that made each one possible.
4. **Share one** aloud or via message to the person involved.
5. **Set a cue** (calendar reminder or sticky note) for the same time tomorrow.

## Prompts to rotate

* A small kindness someone offered today.
* A personal ability you leaned on to solve a problem.
* Something you take for granted at home that worked without notice.
* A place outdoors that improved your mood.
* An obstacle that taught you patience.

## Tracking habit

Keep a short log with date, three gratitudes, and how you shared one. Review weekly to notice patterns and recurring helpers.
