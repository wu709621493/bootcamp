# Carbon Fiber Wings

Carbon fiber wings are where materials science meets flight efficiency.

Unlike traditional aluminum structures, carbon fiber composites combine **high strength** with **low weight**. That means an aircraft can reduce mass without sacrificing structural integrity, improving fuel economy, range, and climb performance.

## Why carbon fiber works for wings

- **Excellent strength-to-weight ratio:** More load-bearing capacity per kilogram.
- **Fatigue resistance:** Better long-term durability under repeated stress cycles.
- **Corrosion resistance:** Less vulnerability to moisture and chemical exposure.
- **Design flexibility:** Composite layups allow engineers to tune stiffness along different directions.

## Tradeoffs and challenges

- **Manufacturing complexity:** Composite layup, curing, and quality control are labor and process intensive.
- **Inspection and repair:** Damage can be less visible than metal cracks and may require specialized NDI methods.
- **Cost:** Raw material and tooling can be expensive, especially for low-volume production.

## Typical applications

- Commercial airliners seeking fuel-efficiency gains.
- High-performance gliders and UAVs where mass savings are critical.
- Modern military aircraft balancing maneuverability and stealth with structural demands.

In short, carbon fiber wings are not just lighter wings—they are engineered structures optimized at the fiber level for performance, longevity, and mission profile.
