# Dawn

The horizon pinks before the birds dare to test the air, and rooftops hold their breath.
Coffee steam waltzes with the first cool breeze, stitching warmth into the quiet.
Alley cats pause their patrol as windows blink awake, trading streetlight secrets for sunbeams.

Each long shadow is an unopened envelope, and every clink of dishes a promise that yesterday's
questions may yet find softer answers. Dawn is the world's small rehearsal, a quiet proof
that starting again is a muscle we practice together.
