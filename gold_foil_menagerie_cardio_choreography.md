# Gold Foil Menagerie Cardio Choreography

In a gym of starlight, foil-bright and gleaming,
We pace the planets, silver lions dreaming.
Each heartbeat cages thunder, each breath sets it free,
A menagerie of motion in the chest’s canopy.

We leap like hares of mercury, we glide like paper swans,
The cardio conductor lifts the baton of dawns.
Cheetahs made of gilded sparks sprint down the spine,
While owls of hammered moonlight keep the tempo fine.

Gold foil feathers shimmer with each measured sweep,
Choreography of pulse where secret rhythms keep.
We bow to the orchestra of lungs that rise and fall,
A menagerie of cardio, bright and mercurial.
