# Estuary for Fake Eels

At the estuary where river forgets its name,
papier-mâché eels glide through silver foam.
They are all costume and current—
stitched from rumor, lacquered with moonlight,
and still the tide accepts them.

Children point from the pier,
laughing as each counterfeit body bends
more gracefully than truth.
A heron watches, unimpressed,
then spears a shadow instead of supper.

By dawn, the fake eels unravel into reeds,
thread, and soaked newspaper.
The estuary keeps no ledger.
It only braids fresh water with salt,
and lets every invented creature try again.
